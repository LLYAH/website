What is a Christian cup?

Everyday cups for water, iced coffee, and the drive to church. Printed with scripture, built to be carried, and sized to fit a standard car cup holder.

Who this is for

  • Daily carry — Water at your desk, iced coffee in the car, a verse either way.
  • Kids and teens — Lighter walls and lids that survive a backpack.
  • Gift buyers — A useful gift under $30 that gets used every day.
  • Church events — Sets for volunteer teams, camps, and retreats.
  • Fitness and outdoors — Straw lids and wide mouths for water through the day.

Sizes, materials, and lids

  • 16oz, 20oz, and 24oz capacities.
  • Double-wall acrylic or stainless depending on the style.
  • Straw, flip, or slide lids, all with silicone seals.
  • Cup-holder friendly base on every size.
  • Hand wash recommended to protect the printed wrap.

Christian Cups questions, answered

What is a Christian cup?

A drink cup printed with scripture or a statement of faith, built for daily carry rather than display.

What sizes do you offer?

16oz, 20oz, and 24oz, with straw, flip, or slide lids.

Will it fit a car cup holder?

Yes. Every size uses a tapered, cup-holder-friendly base.

How should I clean it?

Hand wash with warm soapy water. Dishwashers can fade the printed wrap over time.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days, free over $75.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Funds clean-water wells in Guatemala.
