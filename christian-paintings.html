What is a Christian art print?

The short answer

A Christian art print is a reproduction of an original painting on a faith subject — a scripture passage, a scene, a cross — produced as a giclee, canvas, framed or fine art print. Love & Love YAH prints to order on archival stock and canvas and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Painted scripture, printed properly. Seven print treatments of the same original work — canvas, framed, giclée, watercolor, oil, and fine art — so you can match the finish to the room.

Who this is for

  • Living rooms and entryways — Larger canvas and framed pieces that anchor a wall.
  • Churches and offices — Fine art prints that read as art rather than merchandise.
  • Gift buyers — A framed piece for a wedding, dedication, or new home.
  • Collectors — Archival giclée on cotton rag with a museum border.
  • Kids' rooms — Softer watercolor treatments in smaller sizes.

Treatments, materials, and framing

  • Seven treatments — canvas, fine art, framed, giclée, hand-painted look, oil style, and watercolor style.
  • Archival pigment or 12-color giclée printing for deep blacks and true color.
  • Gallery-wrapped canvas with a 1.25 inch profile, ready to hang.
  • 300gsm cotton rag for fine art and giclée prints.
  • Framed in solid maple or black ash, matted, with hanging hardware.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Paintings and Art Prints questions, answered

What is the difference between your print treatments?

Canvas is gallery-wrapped and ready to hang; fine art and giclée are archival paper prints; framed arrives matted in wood; the hand-painted, oil, and watercolor styles are textured surface prints.

Are these original paintings?

They are prints of original painted work, produced with archival pigment ink so the color holds for decades.

What sizes are available?

Sizes vary by treatment, from 11×14 up to 24×36 inches. Canvas and framed options run larger.

Do prints arrive ready to hang?

Canvas and framed pieces do. Paper prints ship rolled in a rigid tube for your own framing.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

70% goes directly to the artist.
