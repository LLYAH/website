What is a Christian mug?

A verse you read before you are fully awake. Ceramic mugs printed with scripture and short prayers, sized for a full pour and made to survive a dishwasher and a decade.

Who this is for

  • Morning devotionals — Something to hold while you read, and something to read while you hold it.
  • Office and desk — A quiet witness next to the keyboard.
  • Gift buyers — Pairs well with a devotional or a bag of coffee.
  • Church staff and volunteers — Order a set for a team or a welcome table.
  • Wedding and housewarming — A verse for a new kitchen.

Ceramic, sizes, and printing

  • 11oz and 15oz ceramic, glossy white or matte black glaze.
  • Dishwasher and microwave safe, tested through 500 wash cycles.
  • Full-wrap or single-side print, kiln-cured so it will not peel.
  • C-handle sized for a full hand, balanced when full.
  • Boxed for shipping in a molded insert, giftable as it arrives.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Mugs questions, answered

What is a Christian mug?

A ceramic mug printed with scripture, a prayer, or a statement of faith — made for daily use rather than display.

What sizes are available?

11oz and 15oz, in glossy white or matte black.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes. The print is kiln-cured and tested through 500 dishwasher cycles. Microwave safe as well.

Can I order a set?

Yes. Mixed designs can be ordered together for a team, a small group, or a welcome table.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days, boxed in a molded insert.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Funds meals at three community kitchens.
