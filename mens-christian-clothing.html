Men's Christian clothing, built for daily wear

The short answer

Men's Christian clothing at Love & Love YAH covers t‑shirts, long sleeves, hoodies, crewnecks, embroidered caps and performance polos in sizes S through 4XL. Each piece is designed in-house, printed or stitched to order in the United States, and 10% of the purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Most men do not shop often. They find a shirt that fits, wear it until it fails, and go looking for the same one again. That is the standard we design to. The men's line is a short list of pieces built on heavier blanks, with prints placed to survive a work week and a wash cycle, and art that says something true without shouting.

What is in the men's line

  • T-shirts — 4.2 oz ringspun cotton for softness or 6.0 oz for structure, with a slightly tapered body and set-in sleeve. See all tees.
  • Long sleeves — same body, ribbed cuff, sleeve-print options for verse references.
  • Hoodies and crewnecks — 8.5 oz midweight and 10 oz heavyweight fleece with a brushed interior. See all hoodies.
  • Hats — truckers, snapbacks, fitted caps and beanies with true stitched embroidery. See all hats.
  • Polos — moisture-wicking pique for work and course wear. See golf apparel.

Fit and sizing for men

Our men's tees run true to size with a modern cut: a touch of taper through the waist, a shoulder seam that sits on the shoulder, and a body length that stays tucked when you reach. If you prefer a boxier, streetwear silhouette, order one size up and look at the streetwear collection, which is patterned that way from the start. Hoodies are cut generously through the chest and arms so a tee fits underneath without bunching.

  • S to 4XL across tees, long sleeves and fleece.
  • Chest measurement is the reliable number — measure a shirt you already like, flat, armpit to armpit, and double it.
  • Tall builds — size up for length rather than width; our 2XL and up add body length as well as girth.
  • Hats — one-size-adjustable except fitted crowns, which come in sized options.

Designs men actually reach for

The men's designs lean on four ideas. Scripture pieces carry the verse and the reference, set in type clean enough to read across a room. Name pieces put Jesus, Yeshua or Yahushah front and center for men who want no ambiguity about who they follow. Armor and warfare pieces draw on Ephesians 6 and Psalm 91 and tend to be the ones bought by first responders, veterans and men in recovery. Humor pieces keep it light: a line that gets a laugh and then a question.

If you are new to Christian themed clothing, start with a symbol or a short statement. It reads as confident rather than loud, and it goes with the rest of your closet. Scripture-heavy pieces are worth owning too, but they set the tone of an entire outfit.

Print, embroidery, and how long it lasts

Full-color art is printed direct-to-garment, which sits in the fibers and stays flexible instead of forming a plastic panel across the chest. Large solid graphics are screen printed for depth of color. Caps and polos are embroidered with real thread, flat or 3D depending on the design. Nothing here is an iron-on transfer, which is the usual reason a Christian apparel purchase peels after four washes.

  • Wash cold, inside out, tumble dry low. Heat is what cracks a print.
  • Skip the fabric softener — it dulls cotton and clogs wicking finishes.
  • Air dry heavyweights if you want them to keep their shape and length.
  • Spot clean embroidered caps; machine washing collapses the crown.

Buying for a man who does not shop

Hats are the safest gift because they do not need a size. After that, a hoodie in one size up from his usual tee size is nearly always right. If you know his tee size, a 6.0 oz heavyweight in black or heather grey is the most-worn piece in the line. Gift orders can go out with the receipt suppressed, and returns stay open thirty days if the size misses.

Everything is printed or embroidered to order and ships in two to four business days. Free shipping over $75, thirty-day returns, and 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Men's Christian clothing questions, answered

What sizes does men's Christian clothing come in?

Sizes S through 4XL across t-shirts, long sleeves, hoodies and crewnecks. Hats are one-size-adjustable except for fitted crowns.

Do the men's shirts run true to size?

Yes. They have a modern, slightly tapered cut. Order one size up if you want an oversized or streetwear fit.

What fabric are the men's tees?

Ringspun combed cotton, 4.2 oz for a softer drape or 6.0 oz for more structure. Heather colors use a cotton-poly blend.

Are the prints screen printed or transfers?

Direct-to-garment for full-color art and screen print for large solid graphics. Hats and polos are embroidered with thread. We do not use iron-on transfers.

What is the best gift for a man from this collection?

An embroidered cap needs no size, and a hoodie one size above his usual tee size fits almost everyone.

How fast does it ship and can I return it?

Made to order and shipped in two to four business days, free over $75, with thirty-day returns.
