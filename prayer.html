The prayer line

We're here to pray for you.

There is no request too small and no situation too far gone. Our prayer team is made up of volunteers from a dozen churches who take turns covering the line, and every request that comes in is prayed over by name — not filed, not automated, not forgotten.

Call us and speak to someone directly, or send the form below and we'll write back within a day. Ask us to keep it private and we will. Ask us to keep praying and we'll check in every week until you tell us to stop.

How it works
Your request reaches three team members. One prays and replies personally. Nothing is shared outside the team without your permission.
Live prayer calls
Tuesdays and Thursdays, 7:00pm CT on the radio's Morning Prayer channel. Call in or just listen.
Emergencies
We are not a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, call or text 988 first — then call us. We'll still be here.
