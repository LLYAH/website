What is a Christian hat?

The short answer

A Christian hat is a cap, beanie, bucket hat or visor embroidered or printed with a cross, scripture, or the name of Jesus. Love & Love YAH stitches its own designs onto ten blank styles, from structured trucker caps to wide-brim sun hats, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

A hat says it before you do. Embroidered crosses, verse references, and short statements of faith across ten silhouettes, from a structured fitted cap to a wide-brim sun hat.

Who this is for

  • Outdoor work and play — Sun coverage with something worth reading on the front.
  • Teams and volunteers — One embroidered design across a whole serving crew.
  • Everyday wear — Low-profile dad caps and beanies that go with anything.
  • Gift buyers — A one-size gift that fits without guessing a shirt size.
  • Youth ministry — Snapbacks and truckers students pick up on their own.

Silhouettes, materials, and embroidery

  • Ten silhouettes — baseball, dad, fitted, snapback, trucker, mesh-back, bucket, visor, beanie, wide-brim.
  • Cotton twill, brushed canvas, or knit, depending on the style.
  • Adjustable or fitted closures; most styles are one-size-adjustable.
  • Flat or 3D embroidery, not printed, so the mark holds its shape.
  • Spot clean — hats keep their crown better without machine washing.

Christian Hats and Caps questions, answered

What is a Christian hat?

A cap or beanie embroidered with a cross, verse reference, or short statement of faith. Ours are stitched, not printed.

What styles do you carry?

Baseball, dad, fitted, snapback, trucker, mesh-back, bucket, visor, beanie, and wide-brim sun hat.

Are they adjustable or sized?

Most are one-size-adjustable with a strap or snap. Fitted caps come in sized crowns; beanies stretch.

How do I clean an embroidered hat?

Spot clean with cool water and mild soap, then air dry on a form. Machine washing flattens the crown.

How fast does it ship?

Embroidered to order and shipped in two to four business days, free over $75.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Every hat sends a kid to summer camp.
