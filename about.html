About us

Who is Love & Love YAH.

Love & Love YAH is a Christ-centered ministry and shop. We make Holy Spirit inspired apparel and handmade goods, we give away everything we can for free, and we pray with anyone who asks — all to the Glory of God (Yahweh).

Our mission statement

Love your God (Yahweh) with all your heart, strength, and soul. Love your neighbor as yourself. Everything we make, give, and pray exists to point people to Jesus (Yahushah) — The Way, The Truth, and The Life.

Matthew 22:37–39
Who we are

A family serving the body of Christ.

For me and my house we will serve The Lord.

Love & Love YAH began with a simple conviction: the Word belongs out in the open — on what we wear, on what we carry, on the walls of our homes, and in the hands of anyone who needs it.

So we do two things side by side. We design and make faith-forward goods, and we build free resources for anyone including believers, churches, ministries, and bible studies. The shop funds the ministry. The ministry is the point.

We are not any particular denomination but rather followers of The Way, The Truth, and The Life. We believe in one God, one body, and one church — and we want to come alongside you in your walk with The Lord Jesus Christ (Yahushah Hamashiach).

What we believe

The ground we stand on.

One God

Yahweh is God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is love, and love comes from Him.

One Savior

Jesus Christ (Yahushah Hamashiach) is The Way, The Truth, and The Life. Salvation is in Him alone.

One Word

Scripture is our authority. Everything we publish is anchored to it, chapter and verse.

One body

The church is not a building or a brand. We serve believers across every congregation that lifts up His name.

Prayer first

Our designs start in prayer and our prayer line is answered by real people, any hour.

Freely given

Every resource on this site is 100% free to read, print, and share. No paywall, no credit required.
