What is a Christian magnet set?

A magnet set is several coordinated verse magnets sold together, designed to work as a group on one door rather than as one piece competing for attention. Sets are how families work through a memory passage, how churches distribute a sermon series, and how most people buy magnets as a gift.

How sets are used

  • Memory verse progressions — one verse per week through a passage or a season.
  • Sermon series sets — a magnet per week matched to what the church is teaching.
  • Family devotion sets — a verse the whole household reads at the same door.
  • Gift sets — boxed sets for a housewarming, wedding, or new believer.
  • Congregation handouts — one design in bulk, or a coordinated three-piece set.

What is in a set

Sets are built to hang together visually: one palette, one type treatment, and consistent sizing, so a door with six of them on it still looks intentional.

  • Sets of three, six, or twelve — matched palette and layout across the set.
  • Mixed sizing — a 4x6 anchor piece with 2x3 supporting magnets.
  • 20mil flexible magnet — holds paper alongside itself on a fridge door.
  • Boxed for gifting — sets ship in a printed sleeve, ready to give.
  • Works on — fridge doors, lockers, filing cabinets, magnetic boards.

Bulk and ministry ordering

Twelve-piece sets are the most common church order, since one set covers a quarter of weekly verses. Sets can be ordered in quantity with a church name added to the sleeve. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Magnet set questions, answered

How many magnets come in a set?

Three, six, or twelve depending on the design. Twelve-piece sets usually cover a quarter of weekly memory verses.

Are the magnets in a set all the same size?

Most sets pair one 4x6 anchor magnet with smaller 2x3 or 3x4 pieces, so the group reads as a composition rather than a grid.

Can I buy a single magnet from a set?

Some designs are sold individually as well. Sets are priced lower per magnet than buying the same designs separately.

Do sets come gift-wrapped?

Sets ship in a printed sleeve suitable for giving as-is.

Can a church order sets for a sermon series?

Yes. Sets can be ordered in bulk, and the church name can be added to the sleeve. Send your series dates with the order.

Will all twelve fit on one fridge door?

A twelve-piece set is designed to be rotated rather than displayed at once, though most doors hold six comfortably.
