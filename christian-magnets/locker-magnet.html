What is a locker magnet?

A locker magnet is a small verse magnet sized for the inside of a school, gym, or work locker door. It is the one place a student can put scripture that is entirely theirs, seen several times a day, and invisible to everyone else in the hallway.

Who uses them

  • Middle and high school students — a verse inside the door, read between classes.
  • Student athletes — a passage in a gym locker before a game.
  • College dorms — on a mini fridge or the metal frame of a bed.
  • Youth group giveaways — handed out at the start of a school year.
  • Workplace lockers — for shift workers and hospital staff.

Sizes, strength, and fit

Locker doors are thinner steel than a refrigerator, so hold is slightly weaker. These are made small and light on purpose, since a large magnet on a slamming locker door works itself loose.

  • 20mil flexible magnet — light enough to stay put on a slamming door.
  • Sizes — 2x3, 3x4, and 4x6 inches.
  • Full-bleed print — no white border eating into a small surface.
  • Rounded corners — no edge to catch on books going in and out.
  • Sets of three or six — swap a verse weekly through a semester.

Locker magnet questions, answered

Will these hold on a school locker door?

Yes. Locker doors are steel. Keep to 3x4 or smaller if the door gets slammed frequently.

Are magnets allowed in most schools?

Almost always, inside the locker. Check your handbook if anything is posted on the outside of the door.

What size fits a standard locker?

3x4 is the most ordered size and fits beside a mirror or shelf. 4x6 suits full-length lockers.

Do you sell them in sets?

Yes, in sets of three and six, which is how most families and youth groups buy them for a semester of memory verses.

Will the print scratch?

The face is laminated. Books sliding past will not mark it, though a metal buckle scraping it repeatedly eventually will.

Can our youth group get these with our name on them?

Yes. Most layouts allow a ministry or church name added at the bottom edge.
