What is an 11oz mug?

The 11oz ceramic mug is the default coffee mug in most American homes and offices. It holds a standard cup with room to add cream, fits every drip machine and Keurig without adjusting the tray, and is the size people picture when they picture a mug.

Why 11oz is the standard

  • Fits every brewer — clears the drip tray on Keurig, Nespresso, and standard machines.
  • One cup plus cream — a full pour without reaching the rim.
  • Cabinet friendly — stacks and hangs on standard mug hooks.
  • The cheapest to ship — the lowest breakage rate of any mug size.
  • Best print area — a wraparound field of roughly 8.5 by 3.5 inches.

Ceramic, printing, and dimensions

These are white glossy ceramic with the design sublimated into the glaze rather than printed on top of it, which is what makes them dishwasher and microwave safe instead of peeling after a month.

  • White glossy ceramic — standard C-handle shape.
  • Dimensions — 3.8in tall, 3.2in diameter.
  • Sublimated print — fused into the glaze, will not peel or crack.
  • Dishwasher and microwave safe — top rack recommended.
  • Print area — 8.5in by 3.5in wraparound, or one side only.

11oz mug questions, answered

How big is an 11oz mug?

3.8 inches tall and 3.2 inches in diameter, holding about 325ml. It is the standard American coffee mug size.

Will it fit under my Keurig?

Yes. 11oz is the size Keurig drip trays are designed around. 15oz mugs sometimes require removing the tray.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes. The design is sublimated into the glaze rather than printed on the surface, so it will not peel. Top rack is still gentler over years.

Is it microwave safe?

Yes. There is no metallic ink or coating in the print.

11oz or 15oz?

11oz for standard coffee and a machine with a fixed tray. 15oz if you drink large mugs of coffee or tea and refill less.

Can we order these for a church staff gift?

Yes. Any design can be ordered in quantity at one size, which is the most common ministry order in this category.
