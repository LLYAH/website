What is a travel mug?

A travel mug is a sealed insulated mug built for a commute: a lid that will not spill in a bag, a base that fits a car cup holder, and double-wall vacuum insulation that keeps coffee hot for the length of a drive plus the first hour of work.

What matters in a travel mug

  • Cup holder fit — a base under 2.9 inches clears almost every car.
  • Lid seal — a screw lid for a bag, a slider lid for one-handed sipping.
  • Vacuum insulation — double-wall stainless, not a foam sleeve.
  • No sweat — a vacuum wall stays dry on the outside.
  • Grip — a powder-coated body that does not slip in a cold hand.

Construction and sizes

Insulated performance depends on the vacuum between the walls, not on the thickness of the steel. These are 18/8 stainless with a true vacuum gap, which is what produces the six-hour hot rating.

  • Sizes — 12oz, 16oz, and 20oz.
  • 18/8 stainless steel — double-wall vacuum insulated.
  • Hot six hours, cold eighteen — from a preheated or prechilled body.
  • Powder-coated exterior — laser-engraved or UV-printed design.
  • Base under 2.9in — fits standard car cup holders.

Care and gifting

Hand wash the body, and take the lid apart to clean the gasket, which is where every travel mug eventually starts to smell. Travel mug questions, answered

How long does it keep coffee hot?

About six hours. Preheating the mug with hot water for a minute before filling adds noticeably to that.

Will it fit in my car cup holder?

The base is under 2.9 inches, which fits the large majority of cup holders. Check the 20oz against a narrow console.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Hand wash the body to protect the powder coat and the engraved or printed design. The lid can go on the top rack.

Why does my travel mug smell?

The lid gasket. Pull it out weekly and wash it separately. That is where coffee oils collect in every insulated mug.

Screw lid or slider lid?

Screw for a bag, where a leak matters most. Slider for a commute where you drink one-handed.

Will the design wear off?

Laser engraving is permanent. UV printing on powder coat is durable but will show wear at contact points over years of daily use.
