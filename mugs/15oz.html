What is a 15oz mug?

A 15oz mug is the larger everyday ceramic, holding about a third more than the standard 11oz. It suits people who drink one big mug rather than three small ones, and it gives a designer noticeably more print height to work with, which is why longer passages usually land here.

Who reaches for the larger size

  • One-mug mornings — a full pour with cream that lasts through a devotional.
  • Tea drinkers — room for a bag to steep without crowding the rim.
  • Soup and oatmeal — the size doubles as a bowl on a slow morning.
  • Longer passages — more vertical print area for a full verse.
  • Office desks — fewer trips to the kitchen.

Ceramic, printing, and dimensions

Same white glossy ceramic and sublimated glaze print as the 11oz, in a taller and wider body. The one thing to check is your machine: some Keurig drip trays need to come out for a 15oz mug.

  • White glossy ceramic — larger C-handle, comfortable with a full mug.
  • Dimensions — 4.5in tall, 3.4in diameter.
  • Sublimated print — fused into the glaze, dishwasher-proof.
  • Print area — 9in by 4.1in wraparound.
  • Dishwasher and microwave safe — top rack recommended.

Gifting and bulk

The 15oz reads as the more generous gift of the two sizes and is the one most often chosen for pastors, teachers, and Fathers Day. 15oz mug questions, answered

How much bigger is 15oz than 11oz?

About a third more volume, 4.5 inches tall against 3.8, and 4.1 inches of print height against 3.5.

Will a 15oz mug fit under my coffee maker?

Usually, though some Keurig models require removing the drip tray. Measure your clearance at 4.5 inches.

Is the handle bigger too?

Yes. The C-handle is scaled up, which matters when the mug is full and heavier.

Is it dishwasher and microwave safe?

Yes to both. The print is sublimated into the glaze with no metallic content.

Which size holds a longer verse?

15oz. The extra half inch of print height is the difference between a reference and a full passage.

Can I mix 11oz and 15oz in a bulk order?

Yes. Send the design and the count for each size.
