What is a Christian love hoodie?

Love is the word the New Testament uses more than almost any other, and also the word everyday use has worn thinnest. These hoodies put it back in its biblical setting: God is love, love your neighbor, love one another, faith hope and love.

Passages in this collection

  • 1 Corinthians 13 — the love chapter, in full and in excerpt.
  • 1 John 4:8 — God is love, the shortest design here.
  • Mark 12:31 — love your neighbor as yourself.
  • John 13:34 — love one another as I have loved you.
  • Faith Hope Love — from 1 Corinthians 13:13, the most ordered design.

Design treatments

The word invites soft treatments and this collection deliberately does not stay there. It is set in heavy condensed type as often as in script, because the command is a hard one.

  • Script and hand lettering — the warmer treatments.
  • Heavy condensed type — love as a command, not a sentiment.
  • Full passage layouts — 1 Corinthians 13 as a readable block on the back.
  • Small chest type — a single word, understated.
  • Two-color overlays — on the Faith Hope Love designs.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 8.5oz midweight fleece, 9.5oz cotton-rich brushed back, or 12oz heavyweight, in pullover and full-zip. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no fabric softener on fleece. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Love hoodie questions, answered

What is the love chapter?

1 Corinthians 13, Paul's description of what love is and is not. It is the most quoted passage at weddings and the most ordered design here.

Can a full passage fit on a hoodie?

Yes, and better than on a tee. 1 Corinthians 13 is set across the back of a pullover, where the panel is unbroken.

Where does Faith Hope Love come from?

1 Corinthians 13:13, which names the three that remain and calls love the greatest of them.

Are these good for weddings or anniversaries?

1 Corinthians 13 and John 13:34 are the two most ordered designs for both.

Is there a version that is not in script?

Yes. Several designs set love in heavy condensed type, since the biblical command is not a soft one.

Pullover or zip?

Pullover for a full passage or chest design. Zip for a small chest word and easier layering.
