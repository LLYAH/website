What is a Bible verse hoodie?

A Bible verse hoodie carries the actual words of scripture rather than a slogan drawn from them. Every hoodie here prints a complete passage in the American Standard Version with its book, chapter, and verse, sized to be read by the person standing across from you. The ASV is public domain, so whole passages can be printed instead of trimmed down to a licensable phrase. A hoodie also gets worn through the coldest, longest stretch of the year, which is often exactly when the verse on it matters most.

Verses grouped by what you are walking through

  • Courage and fear — Joshua 1:9, Isaiah 41:10, 2 Timothy 1:7, Deuteronomy 31:6.
  • Anxiety and worry — Philippians 4:6-7, Matthew 6:34, 1 Peter 5:7.
  • Grief and loss — Psalm 23, Psalm 34:18, Revelation 21:4.
  • Guidance and decisions — Proverbs 3:5-6, Psalm 119:105, Jeremiah 29:11.
  • Strength and endurance — Isaiah 40:31, Romans 8:28, Galatians 6:9.
  • Identity in Christ — Ephesians 2:10, 2 Corinthians 5:17, John 1:12.

How we set scripture on fleece

Fleece has a nap, so type that reads cleanly on a tee can soften on a hoodie. Passages are set a step larger, hairline strokes are thickened, and long verses break at their natural clauses rather than at the edge of the print area.

  • Full passages, not fragments — the verse is printed through to its end.
  • Reference always included — book, chapter, and verse, so anyone can look it up.
  • Sized up for nap — larger type and heavier strokes than the same verse on a tee.
  • Pullover for long passages — an unbroken chest or back panel keeps the block intact.
  • Ink matched to fabric — water-based on lighter fleece, discharge on darker garments.

Fabric, fit, and care

  • Midweight fleece, 8.5oz — everyday warmth with a soft brushed interior.
  • Cotton-rich brushed back, 9.5oz — denser hand and the sharpest verse type.
  • Heavyweight, 12oz — structured body, dropped shoulder, boxy length.
  • Pullover and full-zip in a unisex cut, sizes XS–3XL, ribbed cuffs and hem.
  • Wash inside out, cold, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach or fabric softener.

Bible verse hoodie questions, answered

What is a Bible verse hoodie?

A pullover or zip hoodie printed with the full text of a scripture passage and its reference. Ours use the American Standard Version and print the passage through to its end rather than cropping it to a phrase.

Which Bible translation do you print?

The American Standard Version (ASV). It is public domain, so we can print complete passages without licensing limits, and its wording stays close to the underlying Hebrew and Greek.

Which verses are available on hoodies?

Verses are grouped by need rather than by book: courage, anxiety, grief, guidance, endurance, and identity. Frequent requests include Joshua 1:9, Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 23, Philippians 4:6-7, Romans 8:28, Proverbs 3:5-6, and 2 Timothy 1:7.

Can you print a verse that is not listed?

Yes. Send the reference along with the hoodie style and color, and we will set it in one of our existing verse layouts. Custom verse requests add two to three business days.

Does the fleece texture affect how the verse reads?

It can, which is why longer passages print at a larger point size on fleece than on a tee. Fine hairline type is avoided entirely on the 8.5oz brushed-back blend.

Pullover or zip-up for a long passage?

Pullover. A full-zip splits the chest panel down the middle, so long passages go on the back or across one panel instead. Short verses and references work well on a zip chest.

What sizes and weights do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 8.5oz midweight fleece, 9.5oz cotton-rich brushed back, or 12oz heavyweight. All are pre-shrunk with ribbed cuffs and hem.

Can churches and small groups order in bulk?

Yes. Any single verse design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes for a memory-verse series, retreat, youth trip, or winter camp. Send the design, size breakdown, and your date.

How do I wash a printed hoodie?

Inside out, cold water, tumble dry low or hang dry. No bleach, no fabric softener on fleece, and never iron directly over the printed verse.
