What is a faith statement hoodie?

A statement hoodie says something in the wearer's own words rather than quoting a passage. On a hoodie the statement gets more room and more months of wear than it does on a tee, which is why Faith Over Fear ends up on more hoodies than shirts once October comes.

Statements in this collection

  • Faith Over Fear — the most requested line in the shop.
  • Redeemed and Chosen — identity statements from Ephesians and 1 Peter.
  • Saved by Grace — the shortest summary of the gospel.
  • Walk by Faith — from 2 Corinthians 5:7, stated rather than quoted.
  • He Is Risen — worn year round, not only at Easter.

Design range and placement

The same three words change meaning with the treatment. A Swiss minimal Faith Over Fear on a heather grey pullover and a graffiti version on black reach entirely different people.

  • Bold streetwear — heavy condensed type, full chest or full back.
  • Minimal and Swiss — small left-chest, understated.
  • Vintage collegiate — arched varsity lettering.
  • Hand-drawn — loose personal letterforms.
  • Back print statements — the largest scale, pullover only.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 8.5oz midweight fleece, 9.5oz cotton-rich brushed back, or 12oz heavyweight, in pullover and full-zip. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no fabric softener on fleece. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Faith statement hoodie questions, answered

What is the difference between a statement hoodie and a verse hoodie?

A statement is written in the wearer's own words. A verse hoodie quotes scripture directly with its reference.

What is the most popular faith statement?

Faith Over Fear, by a wide margin, available here in several design treatments.

Which treatment is most wearable at work?

The Swiss minimal and small left-chest versions. Bold streetwear and graffiti treatments read louder.

Pullover or zip?

Pullover for anything across the chest or back. Zip for layering and for a small chest statement on one panel.

What sizes and weights do you carry?

XS to 3XL in 8.5oz fleece, 9.5oz brushed back, or 12oz heavyweight.

Can a small group order matching hoodies?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes.
