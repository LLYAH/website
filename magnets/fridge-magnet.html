What is a Christian fridge magnet?

The refrigerator door is the most-read surface in most homes. A verse magnet there gets seen more often in a week than a framed print in a hallway does in a month, which is the entire argument for this format.

What people keep on the door

  • Daily verse magnets — one passage, large enough to read while cooking.
  • Prayer list holders — a magnetic clip for a written list of names.
  • Table grace magnets — short blessings placed at child height.
  • Memory verse sets — a series of small magnets swapped weekly.
  • Encouragement magnets — a line for whoever opens the door at 6am.

Sizes, finishes, and strength

Fridge magnets need less holding power than car magnets but more than most people expect, since they are usually asked to hold a school paper or two along with themselves.

  • 20mil flexible magnet — strong enough to hold two sheets of paper.
  • Sizes — 2x3, 3x4, 4x6, and 5x7 inches.
  • Matte or gloss finish — matte hides fingerprints, gloss makes color stronger.
  • Rounded corners — no sharp edge for small hands.
  • Works on — steel fridge doors, filing cabinets, lockers, magnetic boards.

Fridge magnet questions, answered

Will these stick to a stainless steel fridge?

Some stainless finishes are non-magnetic. Test with any existing magnet first. If it slides off, the door is a non-magnetic alloy.

How much can a fridge magnet hold?

A 20mil magnet at 4x6 holds two or three sheets of paper reliably. More surface area means more grip.

What size is best for a memory verse?

4x6 for a full passage, 3x4 for a reference and a short line, 2x3 for single words or a set.

Are these safe around children?

These are flexible sheet magnets, not the small hard button magnets that are a swallowing hazard. Still keep the smallest sizes away from toddlers.

Can I use one on a school locker?

Yes. Locker doors are steel and these hold well on them.

Can we order a set for the whole congregation?

Yes. Any design can be ordered in quantity at one size, which is how most churches handle a sermon series verse.
