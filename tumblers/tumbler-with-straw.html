What is a tumbler with a straw?

A straw changes how much water a person actually drinks. Straw tumblers get sipped continuously rather than picked up and put down, which is why anyone trying to drink more water in a day tends to end up with one.

Why a straw lid

  • You drink more — continuous sipping instead of deliberate drinking.
  • No tipping the cup — usable at a desk, in a car, or on a treadmill.
  • Kinder to teeth — iced coffee and juice bypass the front teeth.
  • One-handed — no lid to open or close.
  • Refillable without removing the lid — on flip-top straw designs.

Straw types and cleaning

Every straw tumbler eventually comes down to cleaning. A straw that cannot be brushed out will grow a film inside within a couple of weeks, so a brush is included with every one of these.

  • Reusable plastic or stainless straw — depending on the size.
  • Cleaning brush included — sized to the straw bore.
  • Silicone gasket lid — removable for washing.
  • Sizes — 20oz, 30oz, and 40oz.
  • Splash-resistant, not leakproof — a straw lid will seep if laid flat.

Care and gifting

Run the brush through the straw twice a week, and pull the lid gasket weekly. Straw tumbler questions, answered

Is a straw tumbler leakproof?

No. Straw lids are splash-resistant but will seep if the tumbler is laid on its side or put in a bag. Use a screw-lid tumbler for a bag.

How do I clean the straw?

With the brush included, twice a week. A straw that is not brushed will develop a film inside within a couple of weeks.

Plastic or stainless straw?

Stainless is more durable and easier to clean. Plastic is softer on teeth and better for anyone who bites a straw.

Can I use it for hot drinks?

It will hold heat, but drinking hot coffee through a straw is not advisable. Use a slider or flip lid for hot drinks.

Is the straw dishwasher safe?

Yes, top rack, but the brush cleans it more thoroughly than a dishwasher will.

What size for all-day water?

30oz is the usual answer. 40oz means fewer refills but stops fitting most cup holders.
