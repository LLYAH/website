What is a 20oz tumbler?

The 20oz is the size most people mean when they say tumbler. It holds a large iced coffee with ice, it fits a car cup holder, and it is small enough to carry in one hand all day. Larger tumblers exist, but they stop fitting in cup holders and start being carried by a handle.

Why 20oz is the common size

  • Cup holder fit — a 2.9in base clears nearly every car console.
  • A full iced coffee with ice — without a second pour.
  • One-hand carry — light enough full, unlike a 30oz or 40oz.
  • Desk and gym — sits on a desk and fits a gym bag pocket.
  • Best print wrap — a tall clean surface with no handle interrupting it.

Insulation and construction

The double-wall vacuum is what does the work. There is no foam or air gap here, which is why the outside never sweats and why an iced drink survives a full workday.

  • 18/8 stainless steel — double-wall vacuum insulated.
  • Cold 24 hours, hot 8 — from a prechilled or preheated body.
  • Press-fit slider lid — clear lid with a closing slider.
  • Powder-coated body — laser-engraved or UV-printed design.
  • No condensation — the vacuum wall stays dry to the touch.

Care and gifting

Hand wash the body to protect the powder coat and the design. 20oz tumbler questions, answered

Does a 20oz tumbler fit a car cup holder?

Yes. The base is 2.9 inches, which fits nearly every standard cup holder. 30oz and 40oz sizes often do not.

How long does ice last?

About 24 hours in a closed tumbler at room temperature, less in a hot car or in direct sun.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Hand wash the body to protect the powder coat and the printed or engraved design. The lid can go on the top rack.

Does it sweat?

No. The vacuum between the walls means no cold reaches the outer surface, so there is no condensation.

Will the design come off?

Laser engraving is permanent. UV printing on powder coat is durable but shows wear at contact points after years of daily use.

20oz or 30oz?

20oz if it needs to fit a cup holder and be carried in one hand. 30oz if you want fewer refills and have somewhere to set it down.
