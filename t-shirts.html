What is a Christian t-shirt?

A Christian t-shirt is a plain-spoken way to carry scripture into an ordinary day. Ours are drawn one design at a time, printed to order, and organized into seven themes so you can find the message before you shop the style.

Who this is for

  • Anyone in a hard season — A shirt you can put on before you feel brave.
  • Churches and small groups — Order a set across mixed sizes for a series, retreat, or baptism Sunday.
  • Youth leaders and camp staff — Designs students actually keep wearing after the week ends.
  • Gift buyers — A Christian gift that is specific rather than generic.
  • Everyday wear — For anyone who would rather preach with a shirt than a speech.

Fabric, fit, and printing

  • Classic cotton, 5.3oz — everyday weight with a relaxed drape.
  • Ring-spun combed, 4.2oz — lighter and softer, holds fine line work.
  • Heavyweight boxy, 7.0oz — structured body and dropped shoulder.
  • Sizes XS–3XL in a unisex cut, pre-shrunk and side-seamed.
  • Water-based, discharge, or plastisol ink, chosen per design.

Christian T-Shirts questions, answered

What is a Christian t-shirt?

A t-shirt printed with scripture, a verse reference, or a statement of faith. Ours pair a specific verse with a specific design so the message reads clearly at a glance.

What sizes and fits do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in three weights. The 5.3oz classic runs true; the 7.0oz heavyweight runs wider through the chest and shoulder.

How should I wash a printed shirt?

Inside out, cold water, tumble low or hang dry. No bleach, and no iron directly on the print.

Can my church order in bulk?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes. Contact us with the design, size breakdown, and your date.

How fast does it ship?

Each shirt is printed to order and leaves in two to four business days. Shipping is free over $75.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Details are on the give-back page.
