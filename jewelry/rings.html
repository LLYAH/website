What is a Christian ring?

A Christian ring carries a verse, a cross, or a vow around the band, where it is read by the person wearing it far more often than by anyone else. This collection covers purity rings, engraved bands, stackable rings, and spinner rings meant to be turned during prayer.

Styles in this collection

  • Engraved verse bands — a full reference etched around the outside or inside of the band.
  • Purity rings — plain and cross-set bands, commonly given at a covenant or dedication.
  • Cross signet rings — a raised cross on a flat face.
  • Spinner and worry rings — an outer band that turns freely, used during prayer or anxiety.
  • Stackable thin bands — 2mm bands meant to be worn two or three at a time.

Sizing, widths, and metals

Ring sizing is the one thing worth getting right before ordering, because engraved bands cannot be resized without cutting through the text. Measure at the end of the day when fingers are largest, and size up a half if the band is 6mm or wider.

  • Sizes 4 to 13 — including half sizes on most styles.
  • Band widths — 2mm stackable, 4mm standard, 6mm and 8mm wide.
  • Stainless steel — the most durable choice and the best for engraved bands.
  • Sterling silver — 925, softer, will show wear marks over years.
  • 14k gold fill — bonded gold that holds color through daily wear.

Christian ring questions, answered

What is a purity ring?

A ring worn as a visible commitment to sexual purity until marriage, usually given at a dedication or covenant service by a parent or church leader.

How do I find my ring size at home?

Wrap a strip of paper around the finger, mark where it meets, and measure the length in millimeters. 52mm is roughly a size 6, 57mm a size 8.

Can an engraved ring be resized?

No. Resizing cuts through the engraving. Confirm the size before ordering, and size up a half on 6mm and wider bands.

What is a spinner ring used for?

The outer band turns freely, which gives hands something to do during prayer, anxiety, or focus. Some people use one rotation per petition.

Can you engrave inside the band?

Yes, up to about 25 characters, which fits most references and short vows.

Will a stainless steel ring hold up to daily work?

Yes. It is the hardest of the three metals here and the best choice for anyone working with their hands.
