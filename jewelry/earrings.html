What are Christian earrings?

Christian earrings carry a cross, a dove, an ichthys, or a small engraved symbol in a form light enough to wear all day. This collection stays deliberately understated: most pieces read as everyday jewelry first and as a faith piece on a second look.

Styles in this collection

  • Cross studs — 4mm to 8mm, the quietest option in the collection.
  • Drop and dangle crosses — a small cross suspended from a stud or hook.
  • Hoops with charms — 12mm to 30mm huggie and open hoops with a removable cross.
  • Symbol studs — dove, ichthys, and olive branch in polished or brushed finish.
  • Threader and huggie styles — minimal shapes for stacked or second piercings.

Metals, posts, and weight

Earring posts sit against skin all day, so every post and hook in this collection is nickel-free surgical grade or solid precious metal. Weight is kept under 3 grams per pair on drop styles so lobes are not pulled.

  • Surgical stainless steel — the safest choice for sensitive or newly healed ears.
  • Sterling silver — 925 posts and backs, bright and polishable.
  • 14k gold fill — a bonded gold layer that will not flake like plating.
  • Backs included — silicone-lined butterfly backs on studs, locking backs on drops.
  • Weight — under 3g per pair on all drop and hoop styles.

Gifting and care

Christian earring questions, answered

Are these earrings safe for sensitive ears?

Yes. All posts are nickel-free surgical stainless steel, sterling silver, or 14k gold fill, which are the three metals least likely to cause a reaction.

What size cross stud should I order?

4mm and 6mm read as a subtle everyday stud. 8mm is clearly visible across a room. 6mm is the most ordered size.

Are the hoop charms removable?

On huggie and open hoop styles, yes. The cross charm slides off so the hoop can be worn plain.

Do you offer clip-on versions?

Not currently. The stud and huggie styles are the lightest options for anyone who cannot wear a full drop.

Will gold fill earrings fade?

Gold fill has a thick bonded gold layer rather than a plated coating, so it holds its color for years with normal wear.

Can these be worn to work or school?

That is what most of the studs are designed for. They read as ordinary jewelry at a glance.
