What is a Christian bracelet?

The short answer

A Christian bracelet carries a verse, a cross, or a short statement of faith on the wrist, where the wearer sees it rather than everyone else. That makes it the piece people reach for during anxiety, recovery, and hard seasons. Ours come as engraved cuffs, beaded strands, and adjustable cord styles. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Styles in this collection

  • Engraved cuff bracelets — a full verse or reference etched across a flat metal band.
  • Beaded stretch bracelets — natural stone and wood beads with a single cross or verse bead.
  • Adjustable cord bracelets — waxed cord with a slide knot, fits most wrists.
  • Chain link bracelets — stainless or gold fill with a small cross charm.
  • Couples and set bracelets — matched pairs for spouses, siblings, or small groups.

Materials and fit

Wrist sizing matters more on bracelets than on any other piece. Measure snugly around the wrist bone and add half an inch for a comfortable cuff.

  • Stainless steel — tarnish-resistant and shower-safe.
  • Sterling silver — 925, softer and brighter, polishes back after tarnish.
  • 14k gold fill — a thick bonded gold layer, not plating.
  • Natural stone and wood beads — 6mm and 8mm, strung on heavy elastic.
  • Sizes — small 6in, medium 7in, large 8in, plus adjustable cord.

Gifting and care

Bracelets are the most common small group and youth ministry gift because they can be ordered as a set at one size and still fit most of the room. Every order ships gift-boxed. Remove before swimming, and keep beads out of prolonged water. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian bracelet questions, answered

What size bracelet should I order?

Measure your wrist with a strip of paper and add half an inch. Under 6.5in order small, 6.5 to 7.5in order medium, over 7.5in order large. Cord styles adjust.

Can a full Bible verse fit on a bracelet?

On a cuff, yes, up to about 40 characters across the band. Longer passages are set as the reference only.

Are the beaded bracelets waterproof?

The beads are, but prolonged soaking will loosen the elastic over time. Take them off before swimming.

Do you make matching sets for small groups?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes. Send the design, size breakdown, and your date.

Which bracelet is best for anxiety or recovery?

Most people choose a cuff engraved with a verse they can read by turning their wrist, commonly Philippians 4:6-7 or Isaiah 41:10.

Will these turn my wrist green?

No. All metals are nickel-free and either solid or gold fill, not plated brass.
