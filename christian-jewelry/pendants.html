What is a Christian pendant?

The short answer

A pendant is the charm itself, sold to hang on a chain you already own or on one added at checkout. Buying the pendant separately is how most people build a layered look or replace a charm on an heirloom chain that has outlasted its original piece. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Styles in this collection

  • Cross pendants — plain, hammered, open-frame, and Jerusalem cross forms.
  • Verse discs — a reference or short phrase etched into a round or oval disc.
  • Medallions — raised relief pieces in a heavier gauge metal.
  • Ichthys and dove pendants — the earliest Christian symbols, in minimal outline form.
  • Locket-style pendants — a hinged front over a small photo or folded verse.

Sizing, bails, and metals

The bail is the loop the chain passes through, and it decides what chains a pendant will fit. Every pendant here lists its bail opening so you can match it to a chain you already have.

  • Bail opening — 3mm to 6mm, fits most standard cable and box chains.
  • Pendant sizes — 12mm small, 18mm medium, 25mm and up for statement pieces.
  • Stainless steel — tarnish-resistant and the most durable everyday option.
  • Sterling silver — 925, heavier feel, polishes back after tarnish.
  • 14k gold fill — a bonded gold layer that holds color far longer than plating.

Gifting and care

Pendants sold on their own are a practical memorial and heirloom gift, since they can be added to a chain that already means something to the wearer. Each ships gift-boxed with the bail size printed on the card. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian pendant questions, answered

Does a pendant come with a chain?

No. Pendants are sold on their own so they can go on a chain you already own. A matching chain can be added at checkout.

Will this pendant fit my existing chain?

Check the bail opening listed on the pendant, from 3mm to 6mm, against the thickness of your chain. Most standard cable chains pass through a 4mm bail.

What size pendant should I choose?

12mm is subtle, 18mm is the most ordered everyday size, and 25mm and up reads as a statement piece.

Can a verse be engraved on the back?

Yes on disc and medallion styles, up to about 20 characters per side.

What is a Jerusalem cross?

A large cross with four smaller crosses in its quadrants, traditionally representing the spread of the gospel from Jerusalem to the four corners of the earth.

Are pendants safe to wear daily?

Stainless steel and gold fill are. Sterling silver is fine daily but will need occasional polishing.
