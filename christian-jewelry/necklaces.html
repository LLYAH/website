What is a Christian necklace?

A Christian necklace is a pendant, cross, or engraved bar worn on a chain as a daily reminder of faith. Ours are made in stainless steel, sterling silver, and 14k gold fill, with scripture references and short phrases engraved rather than printed, so the wording will not wear off with time.

Styles in this collection

  • Cross necklaces — plain, hammered, and open-frame crosses in three metals.
  • Verse bar necklaces — a single reference such as Joshua 1:9 or Psalm 23 engraved on a horizontal bar.
  • Engraved disc pendants — a short phrase or name on a round or oval disc.
  • Layering chains — delicate 16in and 18in cable chains meant to be stacked.
  • Baptism and confirmation pieces — gift-boxed with a card for the date.

Metals, chains, and sizing

Everything is nickel-free and hypoallergenic. Chain length is measured end to end including the clasp, so a 18in chain sits at the collarbone on most adults.

  • Stainless steel — tarnish-resistant, shower-safe, best for everyday wear.
  • Sterling silver — 925, brighter finish, polishes back after tarnish.
  • 14k gold fill — a thick gold layer bonded to brass, far more durable than plating.
  • Chain lengths — 16in, 18in, 20in, and 24in, with a 2in extender on most styles.
  • Engraving — laser-etched into the metal, not surface printed.

Gifting and care

Necklaces arrive in a gift box with a card, which makes them a common choice for baptisms, confirmations, graduations, and Mothers Day. Store dry, remove before swimming or applying lotion, and polish sterling with a soft cloth. Christian necklace questions, answered

What is the most popular Christian necklace?

A simple cross pendant on an 18in chain. After that, verse bar necklaces engraved with Joshua 1:9, Philippians 4:13, or Psalm 46:5.

Will a stainless steel cross necklace tarnish?

No. Stainless steel resists tarnish and can be worn in the shower. Sterling silver will tarnish over time and can be polished back.

What chain length should I order?

16in sits at the base of the neck, 18in at the collarbone, 20in just below, and 24in mid-chest over a shirt. 18in is the most ordered length.

Can you engrave a custom verse or name?

Yes on bar and disc pendants, up to roughly 20 characters per side. Send the exact wording with your order.

Are these good for a baptism or confirmation gift?

Yes. Every necklace ships gift-boxed with a card, and cross and verse pendants are the most requested pieces for both.

Is the jewelry hypoallergenic?

Yes. All three metals are nickel-free, which is what usually causes a reaction with costume jewelry.
