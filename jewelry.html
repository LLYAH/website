What is a piece of Christian jewelry?

Scripture worn close. Bracelets, earrings, pendants, necklaces, and rings finished by hand and engraved with verse, name, or date — small enough to wear every day and solid enough to keep.

Who this is for

  • Everyday wear — Pieces small enough for work, hospital shifts, and school.
  • Baptism and confirmation — An engraved keepsake with a date on the back.
  • Gift buyers — A gift that lasts longer than the season it was bought in.
  • Layering — Chains and stacking bracelets meant to be combined.
  • Milestones — Weddings, graduations, and anniversaries.

Materials, sizes, and engraving

  • Five categories — bracelets, earrings, pendants, necklaces, and rings.
  • Sterling silver, 14k gold fill, and stainless steel, all nickel-free.
  • Hand engraving for verse references, names, and dates.
  • Chain lengths 16 to 24 inches; ring sizes 4 to 13.
  • Boxed with a polishing cloth, giftable as it arrives.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Jewelry questions, answered

What is Christian jewelry?

Jewelry carrying a cross, a verse reference, or an engraved scripture — worn as a daily reminder rather than a statement piece.

What metals do you use?

Sterling silver, 14k gold fill, and stainless steel. All pieces are nickel-free.

Can it be engraved?

Yes. Verse references, names, and dates can be hand engraved on most pendants, rings, and bracelets.

How do I care for it?

Keep it dry, store it in the box, and polish with the included cloth. Remove before swimming or showering.

How fast does it ship?

Made and engraved to order, shipped in two to four business days, boxed with a polishing cloth.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Supports artisan training for women leaving trafficking.
