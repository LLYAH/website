What is a Christian wood sign?

The short answer

Christian woodworking is wood decor made around a faith subject — carved and engraved signs, wall pieces and cut files for makers. Love & Love YAH designs its own artwork, cuts and finishes each sign to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Solid wood signs cut, sanded, and finished in the shop, plus laser and CNC files if you would rather build it yourself. Scripture with grain behind it.

Who this is for

  • Entryways and mantels — A verse in real wood, weight you can feel when you hang it.
  • Weddings and new homes — Names, dates, and a verse, cut to order.
  • Makers — SVG and DXF files for your own laser or CNC.
  • Churches — Larger signs for foyers, classrooms, and welcome areas.
  • Gift buyers — A handmade gift that does not look mass produced.

Wood, finishes, and files

  • Two lines — finished wood signs and décor, and downloadable cut and engraving files.
  • Solid pine, maple, and walnut, sanded smooth and hand finished.
  • Engraved or hand-painted lettering, sealed with a matte topcoat.
  • Hanging hardware installed on every sign.
  • SVG and DXF files tested on common laser and CNC setups.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Woodworking questions, answered

What is a Christian wood sign?

A solid wood sign engraved or painted with scripture, made to hang in a home, church, or office.

What woods do you use?

Solid pine, maple, and walnut, sanded smooth and sealed with a matte topcoat.

What are the cut and engraving files?

Downloadable SVG and DXF files for your own laser or CNC machine, tested on common setups.

Can signs be personalized?

Yes. Names, dates, and verse references can be engraved to order.

How fast does it ship?

Signs are made to order and ship in two to four business days. Files download immediately.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Employs woodworkers in recovery programs.
