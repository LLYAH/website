What is a piece of Christian glasswork?

Glass that catches light and holds a verse. Etched panels, fused crosses, keepsake boxes, ornaments, and leaded suncatchers, each one finished by hand.

Who this is for

  • Windows and sunrooms — Suncatchers and hangings that change through the day.
  • Memorial gifts — Keepsake boxes and etched pieces with a name and date.
  • Christmas — Fused crosses and ornaments, boxed for gifting.
  • Churches — Larger etched panels and wall hangings for quiet spaces.
  • Collectors — One-of-one fused pieces, no two the same.

Techniques, categories, and shipping

  • Seven categories — etched décor, glass blocks, cross ornaments, keepsake boxes, ornaments, wall hangings, and stained glass suncatchers.
  • Sandblast etching and kiln fusing, both done by hand.
  • Leaded stained glass with copper foil seams on suncatchers.
  • Hanging wire or chain included where needed.
  • Boxed in molded inserts and double-walled for shipping.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Glasswork questions, answered

What is Christian glasswork?

Handmade glass pieces — etched, fused, or leaded — carrying a cross, a verse, or a keepsake inscription.

Is each piece unique?

Fused and leaded pieces are one-of-one. Etched pieces follow a set design but each is cut and blasted by hand.

Can a piece be personalized?

Yes. Names, dates, and verse references can be etched on most décor, blocks, and keepsake boxes.

How is glass shipped safely?

Every piece ships in a molded insert inside a double-walled box, and breakage in transit is replaced free.

How fast does it ship?

Made to order and shipped in two to four business days.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Funds apprenticeships for young glass artists.
