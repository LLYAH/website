Daily Bread

Daily Affirmations

I am statements anchored to Scripture, every verse from the New Living Translation. Say them out loud. Nothing here is your own idea about yourself; every line is something God already says is true of you in Christ.

Read one a day, or read a whole theme when you need it.

Unity with Jesus

6 affirmations
I am one with Christ, and He is one with me.

“I pray that they will all be one, just as you and I are one—as you are in me, Father, and I am in you.”

John 17:21 · NLT
I am a branch abiding in the true vine.

“Yes, I am the vine; you are the branches. Those who remain in me, and I in them, will produce much fruit.”

John 15:5 · NLT
I am joined to the Lord, one spirit with Him.

“But the person who is joined to the Lord is one spirit with him.”

1 Corinthians 6:17 · NLT
I am hidden with Christ in God.

“For you died to this life, and your real life is hidden with Christ in God.”

Colossians 3:3 · NLT
I am a member of the body of Christ.

“All of you together are Christ’s body, and each of you is a part of it.”

1 Corinthians 12:27 · NLT
I am part of God’s household, built on Christ Himself.

“So now you Gentiles are no longer strangers and foreigners. You are citizens along with all of God’s holy people. You are members of God’s family.”

Ephesians 2:19 · NLT

Oneness with Jesus

6 affirmations
I am not my own; Christ lives in me.

“My old self has been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me.”

Galatians 2:20 · NLT
I am seated with Him in the heavenly places.

“For he raised us from the dead along with Christ and seated us with him in the heavenly realms because we are united with Christ Jesus.”

Ephesians 2:6 · NLT
I am one with the Son, who is one with the Father.

“The Father and I are one.”

John 10:30 · NLT
I am filled with the fulness of God.

“Then you will be made complete with all the fullness of life and power that comes from God.”

Ephesians 3:19 · NLT
I am His, and He is mine.

“I am my lover’s, and my lover is mine.”

Song of Solomon 6:3 · NLT
I am united with Him in a mystery as deep as marriage.

“This is a great mystery, but it is an illustration of the way Christ and the church are one.”

Ephesians 5:32 · NLT

“But to all who believed him and accepted him, he gave the right to become children of God.”

John 1:12 · NLT

2 Corinthians 5:17 · NLT

“For you have been born again, but not to a life that will quickly end. Your new life will last forever because it comes from the eternal, living word of God.”

1 Peter 1:23 · NLT
I am delivered out of darkness into His kingdom.

“For he has rescued us from the kingdom of darkness and transferred us into the Kingdom of his dear Son.”

Colossians 1:13 · NLT
I am sealed with the Holy Spirit, who guarantees my inheritance.

Freedom from sin

6 affirmations
I am free from the law of sin and of death.

“And because you belong to him, the power of the life-giving Spirit has freed you from the power of sin that leads to death.”

Romans 8:2 · NLT
I am dead to sin and alive unto God in Christ Jesus.

“So you also should consider yourselves to be dead to the power of sin and alive to God through Christ Jesus.”

Romans 6:11 · NLT
I am no longer a servant of sin.

“We know that our old sinful selves were crucified with Christ so that sin might lose its power in our lives.”

Romans 6:6 · NLT
I am free indeed, because the Son has made me free.

“So if the Son sets you free, you are truly free.”

John 8:36 · NLT
I am under grace, and sin shall not have dominion over me.

“Sin is no longer your master, for you no longer live under the requirements of the law. Instead, you live under the freedom of God’s grace.”

Romans 6:14 · NLT
I am a slave to righteousness, and its end is eternal life.

“Now you are free from the power of sin and have become slaves of God. Now you do those things that lead to holiness and result in eternal life.”

Romans 6:22 · NLT

I am loved

6 affirmations
I am loved with an everlasting love.

“I have loved you, my people, with an everlasting love. With unfailing love I have drawn you to myself.”

Jeremiah 31:3 · NLT
I am loved so well that I am called a child of God.

“See how very much our Father loves us, for he calls us his children, and that is what we are!”

1 John 3:1 · NLT
I am loved while I was still a sinner.

“But God showed his great love for us by sending Christ to die for us while we were still sinners.”

Romans 5:8 · NLT
I am chosen, holy, and beloved.

“Since God chose you to be the holy people he loves, you must clothe yourselves with tenderhearted mercy, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience.”

Colossians 3:12 · NLT
I am held; nothing can separate me from His love.

“Indeed, nothing in all creation will ever be able to separate us from the love of God that is revealed in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Romans 8:39 · NLT
I am delighted over; He rejoices over me with singing.

“For the Lord your God is living among you. He is a mighty savior. He will take delight in you with gladness. With his love, he will calm all your fears. He will rejoice over you with joyful songs.”

Zephaniah 3:17 · NLT

“So now there is no condemnation for those who belong to Christ Jesus.”

Romans 8:1 · NLT

“He has removed our sins as far from us as the east is from the west.”
