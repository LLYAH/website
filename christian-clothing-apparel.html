What is Christian clothing?

The short answer

Christian clothing is everyday apparel — t‑shirts, hoodies, hats, polos, streetwear — printed or embroidered with scripture, the name of Jesus, a cross, or a short statement of faith. Love & Love YAH is a Christian clothing store that designs and prints its own line to order in the United States, carries men's and women's fits from XS to 4XL, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Christian apparel works because clothing speaks before you do. A verse across the chest, a cross stitched on a cap, the name of Yeshua on a sleeve: it opens a conversation in a grocery line, at a job site, on the first tee, at a school pickup. That is the whole reason this shop exists. Our designs are drawn to be worn out of the house on a normal Tuesday, not folded in a drawer for camp week.

Christian themed clothing, and choosing what to say

Christian themed clothing falls roughly into four voices, and knowing which one you want makes the shop far easier to navigate. Scripture pieces quote the text directly, chapter and verse included, and they do the most teaching. Name pieces center on Jesus, Yeshua, Yahushah or Yahweh, and they are the most direct. Symbol pieces — a cross, a fish, a crown of thorns rendered as line art — read quietly and pair with anything. Statement and humor pieces say something short and human, which is often what gets a stranger to smile and ask.

If you are buying for someone else, symbol and statement pieces are the safest gift. If you are buying for yourself and want the garment to carry weight, take the scripture route and pick a verse you already return to.

What separates Christian clothing brands

There are a great many Christian clothing companies, and most of them sell the same blank garment with different art on it. The differences worth checking before you buy from any Christian apparel brand are the blank, the print method, the fit range, and where the money goes.

  • The blank — we use ringspun combed cotton at 4.2 to 6.0 oz for tees and 8.5 to 10 oz fleece for hoodies. Heavier is not automatically better; it is warmer and stiffer.
  • The print — direct-to-garment for full-color art, screen print for large solid graphics, and true stitched embroidery on caps and polos. No iron-on transfers.
  • The fit range — XS to 4XL across the core line, with separately patterned women's cuts rather than a shrunken unisex tee.
  • The give-back — 10% of every purchase, not of profit, is donated. Our give-back page lists where it went.
  • The ownership — every design is drawn in-house. Nothing on this site is a licensed reprint of someone else's art.

Sizing, fabric, and care

Our tees run true to size with a modern, slightly tapered body; if you want the boxy streetwear look, size up one. Women's tees are cut with a shorter body and narrower shoulder, so most people wear their normal size. Hoodies are generous through the chest by design, and hats are one-size-adjustable except for the fitted crowns.

  • Wash cold, inside out, tumble dry low. Printed cotton lasts years this way and cracks in months if you run it hot.
  • No fabric softener on performance polos — it clogs the wicking finish.
  • Spot clean embroidered hats rather than machine washing, which flattens the crown.
  • Cotton shrinks about 3% in the first wash; our blends and heavyweights are pre-shrunk.

Christian clothing questions, answered

What is Christian clothing?

Christian clothing is everyday apparel printed or embroidered with scripture, the name of Jesus, a cross, or a short statement of faith. It covers t-shirts, hoodies, hats, polos, streetwear and activewear.

What Christian apparel does Love & Love YAH sell?

Men's and women's t-shirts, hoodies and sweatshirts, embroidered hats, Christian streetwear, golf apparel, graphic tees, funny tees and faith-based basics. Everything is designed in-house and printed to order.

Do you carry both men's and women's Christian clothing?

Yes. Men's fits run S to 4XL and women's fits run XS to 3XL, cut on separate patterns rather than the same unisex blank.

What should I look for in a Christian clothing brand?

Check four things: the weight and content of the blank garment, the print method (direct-to-garment, screen print or true embroidery beats iron-on transfers), the size range offered, and whether the company can tell you where its giving goes.

Is Love & Love YAH a Christian clothing company or a ministry?

Both. We are a Christ-centered ministry that funds its free resources and prayer work through the shop, and 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

How does the sizing run?

True to size with a slightly tapered body on tees. Size up one for an oversized streetwear fit. Hoodies are cut generously through the chest, and most hats are one-size-adjustable.

How fast do orders ship?

Made to order and shipped in two to four business days, free over $75, with thirty-day returns.

The give-back

Every order sends 10% to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.
