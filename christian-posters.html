What is a Christian poster?

Scripture sized for a wall. Archival prints of verses, prayers, and hand-lettered statements of faith, in sizes that fit a standard frame without custom matting.

Who this is for

  • Home and hallway — A verse where the family passes it every morning.
  • Kids' rooms and nurseries — Softer palettes and short, memorable lines.
  • Church and classroom — Larger sizes that hold up across a room.
  • Offices and studios — Quiet typographic prints that are not obviously merch.
  • Gift buyers — Flat-packed, easy to ship, easy to frame.

Sizes, paper, and framing

  • Standard sizes — 11×14, 16×20, 18×24, and 24×36 inches.
  • Archival pigment ink rated for decades without visible fading.
  • Matte 200gsm poster stock or 300gsm cotton rag fine art paper.
  • Unframed and rolled in a rigid tube, or framed in maple or black ash.
  • Standard frame sizes so no custom matting is needed.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Posters & Wall Art questions, answered

What is a Christian poster?

A wall print of scripture, a prayer, or a statement of faith, made to be framed and hung rather than handled.

What sizes do you offer?

11×14, 16×20, 18×24, and 24×36 inches — all standard frame sizes.

What paper do you print on?

Matte 200gsm poster stock, or 300gsm cotton rag for fine art prints, both with archival pigment ink.

Do prints come framed?

Unframed and rolled by default. Framed options in maple or black ash are available on some designs.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days in a rigid tube.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Supports arts programs in under-funded schools.
