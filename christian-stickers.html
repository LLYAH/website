What is a Christian sticker?

Small, cheap, and hard to ignore. Weatherproof vinyl stickers of verses, crosses, and short statements of faith for laptops, water bottles, guitar cases, and bumpers.

Who this is for

  • Students — Laptop and water bottle decoration that says something.
  • Youth ministry — Cheap enough to hand out to a whole room.
  • Small gifts and cards — Slips into an envelope with a note.
  • Cars and gear — Outdoor vinyl that survives sun and a car wash.
  • Church welcome tables — Bulk packs for guests and first-time visitors.

  • 2 to 4 inch die-cut and square formats.
  • Weatherproof vinyl with a UV laminate, rated three to five years outdoors.

Christian Stickers questions, answered

What is a Christian sticker?

A vinyl decal printed with scripture, a cross, or a short statement of faith, made for laptops, bottles, and cars.

Are they waterproof?

Yes. Weatherproof vinyl with a UV laminate, rated three to five years outdoors and dishwasher safe on hard surfaces.

What sizes do you offer?

Roughly 2 to 4 inches, in die-cut and square formats.

Can I order bulk packs?

Yes. Bulk packs are available for events, welcome tables, and youth-group giveaways.
