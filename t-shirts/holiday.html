What is a Christian holiday t-shirt?

Holiday shirts get worn on a handful of days a year and remembered in every photo taken on them. This collection covers Christmas, Easter, Thanksgiving, and Resurrection Sunday, with designs built for family sets, church services, and the specific days a whole room is dressed alike.

Holidays in this collection

  • Christmas — nativity, Immanuel, and the Luke 2 account.
  • Easter and Resurrection Sunday — He Is Risen and empty tomb designs.
  • Thanksgiving — gratitude passages from Psalms and 1 Thessalonians.
  • Good Friday — quieter designs in dark palettes.
  • Advent — one design per week of the season.

Family sets and matching

Most holiday orders are more than one shirt. Designs are drawn so that the same artwork scales correctly from a toddler size to a 3XL without the type becoming unreadable at either end.

  • Scales across sizes — the same design reads at XS and at 3XL.
  • Family and group sets — one design across mixed sizes.
  • Seasonal palettes — deep reds and greens, or muted neutrals.
  • Youth and adult sizing — matched artwork across both.
  • Order lead time — two to four weeks ahead for a holiday date.

Ordering in time

The single most common problem with holiday shirts is ordering late. Everything is made to order, so allow two to four weeks before the date, and longer for a group order across mixed sizes. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Holiday t-shirt questions, answered

When should I order for Christmas?

By the first week of December for a single shirt, and mid-November for a family or group set across mixed sizes.

Do you make matching family sets?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered across mixed adult and youth sizes, and the artwork is drawn to scale correctly at both ends.

What is the difference between an Easter and a Resurrection Sunday design?

Nothing doctrinally. Some churches prefer the name Resurrection Sunday, and both wordings are offered on the same designs.

Do you have Advent designs?

Yes, drawn as a set of four, one per week of the season.

Can a church order these for a Christmas service?

Yes. Send the design, the size breakdown, and your service date, and allow three to four weeks.

What sizes do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, plus youth sizes on holiday designs specifically.
