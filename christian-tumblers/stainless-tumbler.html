What is a stainless steel tumbler?

The short answer

Stainless steel is the reason an insulated cup lasts a decade instead of a season. It does not absorb flavor, it does not leach anything into a drink, it does not crack when dropped, and it can be scrubbed without scratching through to anything underneath. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Why stainless over plastic or glass

  • No flavor absorption — coffee today does not affect water tomorrow.
  • No BPA or plastic leaching — nothing to break down in heat.
  • Survives drops — dents rather than shatters.
  • Holds a powder coat — a grippy exterior that resists chipping.
  • Recyclable — steel is recycled indefinitely without degrading.

Grades, sizes, and finishes

The grade matters. 18/8, also written as 304, contains 18 percent chromium and 8 percent nickel, which is what makes it rust-resistant and food safe. Lower grades pit over time with acidic drinks.

  • 18/8 (304) food-grade steel — rust-resistant and safe for acidic drinks.
  • Sizes — 12oz, 20oz, 30oz, and 40oz.
  • Double-wall vacuum — on every size in this collection.
  • Finishes — powder coat, brushed steel, or matte.
  • Laser engraving — cuts through the coat to the steel beneath, permanent.

Care and gifting

Hand wash and dry, and do not soak overnight, which can seep into the base seam on any insulated cup. Stainless tumbler questions, answered

What does 18/8 stainless mean?

18 percent chromium and 8 percent nickel, also called 304. It is the food-grade standard and resists rust and pitting from acidic drinks.

Will a stainless tumbler make my water taste metallic?

No. 18/8 is inert. A metallic taste usually means a lower-grade steel or a damaged interior.

Can I put hot coffee in it?

Yes. It holds hot drinks six to eight hours, and there is no plastic lining to degrade.

Is stainless dishwasher safe?

The steel is, but hand washing protects the powder coat and design. Lids are top-rack safe.

Can I put it in the freezer?

No. Freezing can damage the vacuum seal. Prechill by filling with ice water for a few minutes instead.

Why did the outside of mine start to rust?

Almost always a chipped powder coat over a lower-grade steel. 18/8 will not rust from ordinary use.
