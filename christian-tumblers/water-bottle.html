What is a Christian water bottle?

The short answer

A water bottle is the item a person carries the most days of the year. Putting a verse on one means the passage shows up on a desk, in a gym bag, at a practice, and on a hospital shift, without anyone having to make a decision about wearing it. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Bottle types in this collection

  • Insulated stainless bottles — double-wall vacuum, cold all day.
  • Single-wall stainless — lighter, no insulation, easier to carry.
  • Glass bottles — with a silicone sleeve, for anyone who tastes plastic.
  • Wide-mouth bottles — fit ice cubes and a bottle brush.
  • Narrow-mouth bottles — drink without spilling while moving.

Sizes, lids, and materials

Mouth width is the decision people underrate. A wide mouth takes ice cubes and cleans easily. A narrow mouth is what you want if you drink while walking or driving.

  • Sizes — 18oz, 24oz, and 32oz.
  • 18/8 stainless or borosilicate glass — BPA-free throughout.
  • Screw, flip, or straw lid — screw lids are the only truly leakproof option.
  • Wide or narrow mouth — wide for ice, narrow for drinking on the move.
  • Powder-coated or sleeved — grip that survives being dropped.

Care and gifting

Wash the body by hand and brush the interior weekly, especially on insulated bottles where nothing dries fully overnight. Water bottle questions, answered

Which lid is actually leakproof?

A screw lid. Flip and straw lids are splash-resistant but will seep in a bag laid on its side.

Wide mouth or narrow mouth?

Wide if you use ice cubes or want easy cleaning. Narrow if you drink while walking, running, or driving.

Insulated or single-wall?

Insulated keeps water cold all day but weighs more. Single-wall is lighter and cheaper if the bottle refills often.

Do glass bottles break easily?

Borosilicate glass in a silicone sleeve survives normal drops. It will break on concrete. It is for people who taste metal or plastic.

How do I clean the inside?

A bottle brush weekly. Insulated bottles never fully air dry, which is where odor comes from.

Yes. Any design can be ordered in quantity at one size, with a team or ministry name added to most layouts.
