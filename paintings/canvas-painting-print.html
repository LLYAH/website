What is a canvas painting print?

A canvas painting print reproduces an original painting onto woven canvas and stretches it over wood, so the reproduction carries the weave and the scale of the piece it came from. Against a paper print of the same image, the canvas reads as a painting from across the room.

What reproduces well on canvas

  • Oil and acrylic originals — brushwork holds its texture against the weave.
  • Large-scale scripture pieces — type gains weight at 24x36 and above.
  • Landscape and biblical scenes — depth reads better without glass in front of it.
  • Muted and earth palettes — canvas warms color slightly, which suits them.
  • Anything hung opposite a window — no glass means no reflection.

Construction and sizes

Every canvas is stretched over kiln-dried solid wood at a 1.25 inch gallery depth and finished with a protective coat that guards against dust and UV without adding gloss.

  • Sizes — 12x16, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x36 inches.
  • 1.25in gallery depth — standard profile, sits close to the wall.
  • Kiln-dried stretcher bars — resist warping and corner slack.
  • Matte protective coat — UV and dust resistant, no added glare.
  • Ready to hang — wire or sawtooth fitted before shipping.

Canvas painting print questions, answered

Is this an original painting?

No. It is a reproduction of an original painting, printed onto canvas and stretched over wood. Originals are not sold through this collection.

Does it look like a real painting?

From a normal viewing distance, close. The canvas weave and the absence of glass are what sell it. Up close you can see it is a print.

Does it need a frame?

No. It arrives stretched and ready to hang. A floater frame can be added later for a finished edge.

What size should I order?

24x36 over a sofa, 18x24 over a console, 16x20 for a gallery wall alongside smaller pieces.

How do I clean it?

Dry microfiber cloth only. Water or cleaner will permanently mark the coated surface.

Will it warp over time?

Not with kiln-dried bars. If the canvas ever loosens in humidity, tapping the corner wedges re-tensions it.
