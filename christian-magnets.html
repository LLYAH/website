What is a Christian magnet?

The verse you see forty times a day without meaning to. Fridge and locker magnets printed with scripture, short prayers, and reminders worth keeping at eye level.

Who this is for

  • Kitchens — A verse on the fridge, read every time someone reaches for milk.
  • Students — Locker magnets that hold a schedule and say something.
  • Care packages — Light, flat, and cheap to mail anywhere.
  • Church outreach — Handouts with a verse and no sales pitch.
  • Offices — Whiteboard and cabinet magnets that hold real paper.

Sizes, backing, and strength

  • 2×3, 3×3, and 4×6 inch formats.
  • Flexible or rigid backing depending on size and holding strength.
  • Printed and laminated so kitchen humidity will not lift the ink.
  • Strong enough to hold several sheets of paper on a steel fridge.
  • Singles and multi-packs for events and giveaways.

Everything is made to order and ships in two to four business days, with free shipping over $75 and returns open for thirty days. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Christian Magnets questions, answered

What is a Christian magnet?

A printed magnet carrying scripture or a short prayer, made for a fridge, locker, or filing cabinet.

What sizes do you offer?

2×3, 3×3, and 4×6 inches, in flexible or rigid backing.

Will it hold paper?

Yes. The rigid-backed sizes hold several sheets on a standard steel fridge door.

Can I order multi-packs?

Yes. Multi-packs are available for care packages, events, and outreach handouts.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days, flat in a rigid mailer.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.
