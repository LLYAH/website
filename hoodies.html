What is a Christian hoodie?

A Christian hoodie carries the same verse as a tee with more weight behind it — the piece you reach for on a cold morning, a long drive, or a late night. Seven themes, printed to order in heavyweight fleece.

Who this is for

  • Cold-weather wear — Brushed fleece inside, ribbed cuffs, built to be lived in.
  • Youth groups and retreats — One design across mixed sizes for a whole van full of students.
  • Worship and tech teams — Quiet dark colorways that read well on a platform or backstage.
  • Gift buyers — A heavier gift than a tee, and one that gets worn for years.
  • Everyday layering — Pullover or full-zip under a jacket, through the whole season.

Fleece, fit, and printing

  • Midweight pullover, 8.0oz — cotton-poly fleece, everyday warmth.
  • Heavyweight pullover, 10.0oz — dense fleece with a boxier body.
  • Full-zip, 8.5oz — metal hardware and split kangaroo pockets.
  • Sizes XS–3XL unisex, ribbed cuffs and hem, double-needle seams.
  • Screen or discharge printing so the graphic stays soft on fleece.

Christian Hoodies & Sweatshirts questions, answered

What is a Christian hoodie?

A hooded sweatshirt printed with scripture or a statement of faith. Ours use the same seven themes as our tees, drawn for a larger print area.

What weights do you offer?

An 8.0oz midweight pullover, a 10.0oz heavyweight pullover, and an 8.5oz full-zip. All unisex, XS to 3XL.

Will the print crack on fleece?

Not with normal care. Wash inside out in cold water and tumble low. Avoid ironing the graphic.

Can we order for a whole group?

Yes. Single designs can be ordered across mixed sizes for youth groups, teams, and retreats.

How fast does it ship?

Printed to order and shipped in two to four business days, free over $75.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

The give-back

Funds winter coats for shelters in six cities.
