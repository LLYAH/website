Today · August 25

The ground you have been praying over is not barren. What you planted in the quiet season is rooting deeper than you can see, and this is the month the roots find water. Do not trade a slow harvest for a fast one.

Prayer

Carry it today

Name one thing you have been tempted to abandon early. Give it one more week of prayer before you decide.
