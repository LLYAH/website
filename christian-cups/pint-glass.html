What is a pint glass?

A pint glass is a 16oz tapered tumbler, the most common everyday drinking glass in an American kitchen. It stacks, it fits every cabinet, it holds a full can with room for ice, and its straight tapered wall gives a printed or etched design more usable surface than almost any other glass shape.

Everyday uses

  • Water and iced tea — the standard household glass.
  • Soda and juice — holds a full 12oz can plus ice.
  • Table settings — stacks in a cabinet, matches across a set.
  • Gift pairs — two glasses etched with a date and a passage.
  • Church and hall use — durable enough for fellowship hall service.

Glass, printing, and dimensions

Designs on these are either sandblast etched into the glass or fired ceramic ink, both of which are permanent. Neither is a surface print that will wash off after a few cycles.

  • 16oz capacity — 5.75in tall, 3.5in rim diameter.
  • Heavy tempered base — resists chipping on a hard counter.
  • Sandblast etched or fired ink — permanent, dishwasher safe.
  • Stackable — nests in a standard cabinet.
  • Sold singly or in sets of four. — undefined

Pint glass questions, answered

How much does a pint glass hold?

16oz to the rim, which is a full 12oz can with room for ice.

Is the design dishwasher safe?

Yes. Designs are sandblast etched or fired ceramic ink, both permanent. Nothing here is a surface sticker or decal.

Do these stack?

Yes. The tapered shape nests, which is why this shape is standard in most kitchens.

Can a name and date be etched?

Yes on most designs, which is why these are a common wedding and anniversary gift.

Are they sold in sets?

Singly or in sets of four. Sets are priced lower per glass.

Will the base chip?

The base is heavy tempered glass and resists chipping better than a thin-walled tumbler on a hard counter.
