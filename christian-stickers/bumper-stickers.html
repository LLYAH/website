What is a Christian bumper sticker?

A Christian bumper sticker is outdoor-rated vinyl printed with a verse, a symbol, or a short line of faith, made to survive weather, car washes, and years of sun on a rear bumper or window. The whole point is legibility at a stoplight, so the type on these is set larger and heavier than on an indoor sticker.

What people put on their car

  • Short verse references — Joshua 1:9 or Philippians 4:13, readable in one glance.
  • Ichthys and cross symbols — the oldest and most recognized Christian marks.
  • Statement lines — brief declarations of faith, kept to five words or fewer.
  • Ministry and church stickers — a congregation name with a city.
  • Encouragement lines — aimed at the driver behind you, not at you.

Material, weather, and application

Outdoor vinyl and indoor sticker paper are not the same product. These use 4mil white vinyl with a UV laminate over the print, which is what keeps the ink from chalking out after two summers.

  • 4mil outdoor vinyl — the standard for automotive use.
  • UV laminate — protects against sun fade and car wash brushes.
  • Weatherproof — rated for three to five years outdoors.
  • Sizes — 3x8, 4x11, and 5x15 inches.
  • Removable without residue — lifts clean with heat from a hair dryer.

Applying it correctly

Clean the surface with rubbing alcohol, not soap, and apply above 50 degrees. Christian bumper sticker questions, answered

Are these safe for car paint?

Yes. They apply to painted bumpers, glass, and plastic, and lift off with gentle heat without pulling paint or leaving adhesive.

How long will one last outdoors?

Three to five years with the UV laminate, depending on how much direct sun the car sees.

Will it survive a car wash?

Yes, including brush washes, once it has cured for 24 hours after application.

What size is best for a rear bumper?

4x11 inches is the most ordered size and is legible from about two car lengths back. 3x8 suits a window.

Can I put one on my rear windshield?

Yes, on the outside of the glass. These are not designed for inside-glass reverse-read application.

Can churches order these in bulk?

Yes. Any design can be ordered in quantity, with a church name and city added to most layouts.
