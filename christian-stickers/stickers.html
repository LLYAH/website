What is a Christian sticker?

A Christian sticker puts scripture on the things a person carries all day: a laptop lid, a water bottle, a notebook, a phone case, a Bible cover. It is the smallest and cheapest way to keep a verse in the sight line, and the format students and youth groups buy most.

Where people put them

  • Laptops and tablets — die-cut singles that survive being shoved in a bag.
  • Water bottles and tumblers — waterproof vinyl that holds through hand washing.
  • Bible covers and journals — small 2in stickers for margins and covers.
  • Phone cases — under a clear case, protected indefinitely.
  • Youth group giveaways — sheets and packs handed out at events.

Types, sizes, and durability

Not every sticker is the same build. Die-cut singles are cut to the shape of the art with a matching backing. Kiss-cut stickers sit on a square backing, which is easier to peel and better for handouts.

  • Die-cut vinyl singles — cut to the outline of the artwork.
  • Kiss-cut singles — shaped sticker on a square backing, easiest to peel.
  • Sticker sheets — six to twelve small designs on one page.
  • Sizes — 2in, 3in, 4in, and 5.5in on the longest edge.
  • Matte or gloss laminate — waterproof and scratch-resistant.

Christian sticker questions, answered

Are these stickers waterproof?

Yes. The vinyl and laminate are waterproof and hold up to hand washing on a bottle. Avoid the dishwasher, which lifts any sticker eventually.

Will a sticker damage my laptop?

No. It peels off cleanly from anodized aluminum and plastic lids without leaving residue.

What is the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut?

Die-cut is trimmed to the shape of the art. Kiss-cut leaves the design on a square backing, which is easier to peel and better for handing out.

What size fits a water bottle?

3in is the most ordered size for bottles and tumblers. 2in suits phone cases and Bible margins.

Matte or gloss?

Gloss makes color pop and resists scuffing. Matte hides fingerprints on a laptop lid.

Can we order a pack for a youth group?

Yes. Any design can be ordered in quantity, and multi-design sheets are the most economical per person.
