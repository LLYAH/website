What is a Christian poster?

A poster is an unframed print meant to go up fast, with tape, tack, or a magnetic hanger. That makes it the format of choice for dorm rooms, youth rooms, classrooms, and anywhere the art will change out before the paint does. Ours are printed on heavier stock than typical posters so they lie flat instead of curling at the corners.

Who these are for

  • Students — dorm and apartment walls, where nothing can be nailed.
  • Youth rooms and student ministries — bold type that reads across a full room.
  • Sunday school and classrooms — memory verses and books-of-the-Bible charts.
  • Home offices — a single verse in the sight line above a desk.
  • Anyone renting — wall art that leaves no holes behind.

Paper, sizes, and hanging

Poster paper is a compromise between weight and cost. At 200gsm these are noticeably stiffer than a standard 150gsm poster, which is what keeps the corners down without a frame.

  • Sizes — 11x17, 18x24, and 24x36 inches.
  • 200gsm matte stock — low glare and stiff enough to hang unframed.
  • Optional 3mm white border — for anyone who plans to frame it later.
  • Ships rolled — in a rigid tube at 18x24 and above.
  • Hanging — poster tape, tack, magnetic rails, or a standard frame.

Christian poster questions, answered

What is the difference between a poster and a print?

Weight and intent. Posters here are unframed 200gsm stock meant to be hung directly. Prints are archival paper or canvas meant to be framed or stretched.

How do I hang a poster without damaging the wall?

Poster putty at all four corners, removable mounting strips, or a magnetic poster hanger on the top and bottom edge. All three come off clean.

What size poster is best for a dorm room?

18x24. It reads from across the room without dominating a small wall. 24x36 is better for youth rooms and larger spaces.

Will the poster arrive creased?

No. Posters 18x24 and above ship rolled in a rigid tube. Let it lie flat under a book overnight if it holds any curl.

Can I frame these later?

Yes. All sizes are standard frame sizes, and the optional 3mm border gives a frame something to bite.

Can I order the same design in bulk for a classroom?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered in quantity at one size. Contact us with the design, count, and your date.
