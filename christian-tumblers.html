What is a Christian tumbler?

Insulated stainless tumblers printed with scripture. Cold for twenty-four hours, hot for eight, and quiet enough to take into any meeting.

Who this is for

  • Long shifts — Nurses, teachers, and drivers who need one cup to last a day.
  • Commuters — Sealed lids and a base that fits the holder.
  • Gift buyers — A gift that feels substantial in the hand.
  • Church staff — Branded sets for teams and volunteer appreciation.
  • Outdoor work — Powder-coated shells that take a knock.

Insulation, sizes, and finish

  • 20oz and 30oz double-wall vacuum-insulated stainless steel.
  • Cold 24 hours, hot 8 hours in normal room conditions.
  • Powder-coated shell with a laser-etched or printed graphic.
  • Press-fit or screw lids with a slide closure and straw option.
  • Hand wash to protect the coating and the etch.

Christian Tumblers questions, answered

What is a Christian tumbler?

An insulated stainless tumbler printed or etched with scripture, made for all-day use.

What sizes are available?

20oz and 30oz, both double-wall vacuum insulated.

How long does it hold temperature?

Cold up to twenty-four hours and hot up to eight, in normal room conditions.

Can it go in the dishwasher?

Hand wash is recommended. Dishwasher heat can dull the powder coat and the graphic.
