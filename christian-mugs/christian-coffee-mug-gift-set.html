What is a Christian mug gift set?

A mug gift set pairs a mug with the things that make it a complete present rather than a mug in a box: a coaster, a coffee or tea sampler, a verse card, or a second matching mug. It is what most people actually want when they buy a mug as a gift.

What comes in a set

  • Mug and coaster sets — a matching wood or cork coaster.
  • His and hers pairs — two mugs in one palette, for weddings and anniversaries.
  • Mug and coffee sets — a 12oz bag of ground coffee with the mug.
  • Tea sets — a mug, an infuser, and a loose leaf sampler.
  • Devotional sets — a mug with a verse card deck for quiet time.

Packaging and presentation

Every set ships in a rigid printed box with a molded insert, not loose in a padded mailer, so it can be handed over as it arrives without any additional wrapping.

  • Rigid printed gift box — with a molded insert holding each piece.
  • Blank card included — write the note before you hand it over.
  • Ships gift-ready — no wrapping needed.
  • Direct-ship option — send it straight to the recipient with your message.
  • Sets of two or four mugs — for households and small groups.

Occasions and bulk

Mug sets are ordered most for weddings, pastor appreciation, teacher gifts, and Christmas. Sets can be ordered in quantity for a staff or a whole ministry team. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Mug gift set questions, answered

What comes in a mug gift set?

It varies by set: a mug plus a coaster, a coffee or tea sampler, a verse card deck, or a second matching mug. Each set lists its contents.

Does it arrive ready to give?

Yes. Sets ship in a rigid printed box with a molded insert and a blank card. No additional wrapping is needed.

Can you ship it directly to the recipient?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout and add your message, and we will include it with the card.

Can I mix designs in a two-mug set?

Yes on the his and hers pairs, where two coordinating designs in one palette are the more common choice.

Is the coffee included fresh?

Coffee is added at packing, not stored with the mugs, so it ships within days of roasting.

Can we order sets for a whole church staff?

Yes. Sets can be ordered in quantity. Send the count and your date, and allow an extra week for larger orders.
