What is a Christian baseball cap?

The baseball cap is the default hat: a curved brim, six panels, and an adjustable back. A verse or a cross embroidered on the front panel reads at conversational distance without announcing itself the way a shirt does, which is why a lot of people who would not wear a faith tee will wear a faith cap.

Styles in this collection

  • Unstructured dad caps — a soft low crown that sits down on the head.
  • Structured six-panel — a firm front panel that holds its shape.
  • Curved brim — pre-curved, the default on every cap here.
  • Embroidered front — raised or flat stitch on the center panel.
  • Adjustable strap — buckle, slide, or velcro depending on the style.

Fabric, fit, and embroidery

Structure decides the look more than color does. An unstructured cap collapses into a soft shape and looks worn-in immediately. A structured cap stands up off the forehead and reads sharper.

  • Cotton twill or washed chino — washed cotton fades softer over time.
  • Structured or unstructured crown — firm front panel or soft.
  • Adjustable 21in to 24in — fits most adults.
  • Flat or 3D puff embroidery — 3D raises the design off the panel.
  • Six panels with brass or plastic slide. — undefined

Care and bulk

Hand wash and air dry on a bowl or a cap form. A dryer will shrink the sweatband and warp the brim on any cap. Baseball cap questions, answered

Structured or unstructured cap?

Unstructured sits soft and low, like a broken-in dad cap. Structured stands up off the forehead and holds a sharper front panel.

What size are these?

One size adjustable, fitting roughly 21 to 24 inches of head circumference. Fitted sizes are a separate style.

How do I wash a baseball cap?

Hand wash in cool water and air dry over a bowl to hold the crown shape. A dryer shrinks the band and warps the brim.

What is 3D puff embroidery?

Stitching over a foam backing so the design stands off the panel. It suits bold letterforms and looks flat and muddy on fine detail.

Will the brim stay curved?

Pre-curved brims hold their shape. Bending it further while damp and letting it dry sets a deeper curve.

Can we order caps for a team or camp?

Yes. Any design can be run in quantity with a team, church, or camp name added.
