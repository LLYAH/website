What is a canvas print?

A canvas print is an image printed onto woven cotton canvas and stretched over a wooden frame, so it hangs finished without glass or a surround. It has no reflection, which is the practical reason it wins in kitchens, hallways, and any room with a window opposite the wall.

Why canvas over paper

  • No glare — nothing to reflect a lamp or a window back at you.
  • Ready to hang — stretched, wired, and finished on arrival.
  • Nothing to break — no glass, which matters in nurseries and kids rooms.
  • Texture — the weave softens hard type and suits painted-look artwork.
  • Scale without weight — a 24x36 canvas weighs a fraction of the same size framed.

Construction and sizes

A canvas is only as good as its stretcher bars. Ours are kiln-dried solid wood, which resists the warping that makes a cheap canvas bow away from the wall after a season.

  • Sizes — 12x16, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x36 inches.
  • 1.25in stretched depth — a standard gallery profile.
  • Kiln-dried solid wood bars — resists warping and corner slack.
  • Mirrored or solid edge wrap — the image continues or a matched color runs around the sides.
  • Hanging hardware — sawtooth or wire fitted before shipping.

Care and gifting

Dust with a dry cloth only, never a wet one, and keep canvas out of direct all-day sun. Canvas print questions, answered

Does a canvas print need a frame?

No. It arrives stretched over wood and ready to hang. A floater frame can be added later if you want a finished edge.

What is edge wrap?

How the sides are handled. Mirrored wrap continues the image around the edge. Solid wrap runs a matched color instead, which keeps the artwork uncropped on the face.

How do I clean a canvas print?

Dust with a dry microfiber cloth. Never use water or cleaner, which will mark the coated surface permanently.

Will a canvas sag over time?

Not with kiln-dried bars. If a canvas ever loosens in high humidity, tapping the corner wedges back in re-tensions it.

Canvas or framed print for a nursery?

Canvas. There is no glass to break and it weighs far less on the wall.

What size canvas over a sofa?

24x36 for a standard three-seat sofa, or two 18x24 canvases hung with three inches between them.
