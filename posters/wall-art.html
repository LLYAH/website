What is Christian wall art?

Christian wall art is scripture, imagery, or a statement of faith made to hang in a home, office, church, or classroom. This collection covers framed and unframed prints, canvas wraps, and multi-piece sets, printed on archival stock so the color holds for decades rather than fading through a few summers of window light.

Where people hang it

  • Entryways — a house blessing or Joshua 24:15 as the first thing guests read.
  • Kitchens and dining rooms — table graces and short verses at eye level over a counter.
  • Nurseries and kids rooms — gentle passages set in soft palettes, often as a set of three.
  • Home offices and studies — longer passages meant to be read while sitting still.
  • Church lobbies and classrooms — large-format pieces sized to be legible across a room.

Sizes, materials, and framing

Size is the decision people most often get wrong. As a rule, art should span roughly two thirds the width of the furniture beneath it, and hang with its center at 57 inches from the floor.

  • Sizes — 8x10, 11x14, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x36 inches.
  • Archival matte paper — 200gsm, no glare, best for framing under glass.
  • Canvas wrap — 1.25in stretched depth, ready to hang without a frame.
  • Framed prints — solid wood in black, white, or natural oak with a mat.
  • Pigment inks — rated for decades of color hold indoors, away from direct sun.

Gifting and hanging

Wall art is a common housewarming, wedding, and new-baby gift because it fits a room rather than a person. Christian wall art questions, answered

What size wall art should I buy?

Measure the furniture below it and choose a piece about two thirds that width. Over a standard sofa that is usually 24x36, over a console 16x20.

How high should I hang it?

Center the piece at 57 inches from the floor, which is standard gallery height. Over furniture, leave 6 to 10 inches of gap above the top edge.

Framed print or canvas?

Framed reads more formal and protects the print under glass. Canvas has no glare and works better in kitchens, bathrooms, and rooms with strong light.

Will the print fade in a sunny room?

Pigment inks resist fading for decades indoors, but no print should hang in direct unfiltered sunlight all day. Use a sheer or reposition off the sun line.

Do you make sets for a gallery wall?

Yes. Several designs come as coordinated sets of three, sized 8x10 or 11x14, meant to hang with 2 to 3 inches between frames.

Can churches order large-format pieces?

Yes. 24x36 is the largest stock size, and larger custom sizes can be quoted for lobbies and classrooms.
