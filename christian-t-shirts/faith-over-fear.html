What is a Faith Over Fear t-shirt?

A Faith Over Fear shirt puts one decision on the front of your body: that trust gets the last word, not fear. The phrase comes from 2 Timothy 1:7 — “For God hath not given us a spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind” (American Standard Version). It is short enough to read at a glance and clear enough to start a conversation, which is why it has become one of the most requested Christian apparel phrases for church small groups, youth ministry, worship teams, hospital waiting rooms, first days of chemo, first days of sobriety, and first days at a new job.

We print the same four words eighteen ways because people are not one way. Every design on this page is drawn from scratch, printed to order, and available in seven unisex sizes from XS to 3XL across three fabric weights.

Who this collection is for

  • Anyone walking through a hard season — a diagnosis, a layoff, a move, a court date. A shirt you can put on before you feel brave.
  • Churches and small groups ordering a set for a series on courage, baptism Sunday, or a retreat.
  • Youth leaders and camp staff who need something students actually want to wear after the week ends.
  • Gift buyers looking for a Christian gift that is specific rather than generic — birthdays, graduations, get-well boxes.
  • Everyday wear for anyone who would rather preach with a t-shirt than a speech.

Fabric, fit, and printing

Every design ships in the same three bodies, so you can pick the feel independently of the artwork. All three are unisex, pre-shrunk, side-seamed, and taped at the neck.

  • Classic cotton, 5.3oz — everyday weight with a relaxed drape. The safe pick if you are buying for someone else.
  • Ring-spun combed, 4.2oz — lighter and softer, with a finer surface that holds small type and thin line work.
  • Heavyweight boxy, 7.0oz — structured body and dropped shoulder for a modern, wider cut.

Sizes run XS through 3XL. Prints are water-based or discharge ink on light bodies and plastisol on dark ones, chosen per design so the ink sits in the fabric rather than on top of it. Orders print and ship in two to four business days, and returns stay open for thirty.

Faith Over Fear questions, answered

What Bible verse is Faith Over Fear based on?

2 Timothy 1:7. In the American Standard Version it reads, “For God gave us not a spirit of fearfulness; but of power and love and discipline.” Several designs also nod to Isaiah 41:10, “Fear not, for I am with you.”

How many Faith Over Fear designs are there?

Eighteen, each with its own product page, color range, and price. The words are identical; the drawing, palette, and print method are not.

What sizes and fits do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in three weights. If you are between sizes, the 5.3oz classic runs true and the 7.0oz heavyweight runs wider through the chest and shoulder.

How should I wash a printed Christian shirt?

Inside out, cold water, tumble low or hang dry. No bleach and no iron directly on the print. Handled that way, these prints outlast the trend they were drawn in.

Can my church or small group order in bulk?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes. Contact us with the design, the size breakdown, and your date and we will confirm timing.

Where does the give-back money go?

10% of every order is given away — clean water wells, youth camp scholarships, meals, mental-health support, street outreach, and the arts, depending on the design. Details are on the give-back page.

How fast does it ship, and can I return it?

Each shirt is printed to order and leaves in two to four business days. Shipping is free over $75, and returns are open for thirty days.
