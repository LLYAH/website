What is a Bible verse t-shirt?

A Bible verse t-shirt carries the actual words of scripture, not a paraphrase or a slogan drawn from one. Every shirt in this collection prints a complete passage in the American Standard Version with its book, chapter, and verse, set at a size meant to be read by the person standing across from you. The ASV is public domain, so we can print whole passages rather than trimming them down to a licensable phrase.

Verses grouped by what you are walking through

  • Courage and fear — Joshua 1:9, Isaiah 41:10, 2 Timothy 1:7, Deuteronomy 31:6.
  • Anxiety and worry — Philippians 4:6-7, Matthew 6:34, 1 Peter 5:7.
  • Grief and loss — Psalm 23, Psalm 34:18, Revelation 21:4.
  • Guidance and decisions — Proverbs 3:5-6, Psalm 119:105, Jeremiah 29:11.
  • Strength and endurance — Isaiah 40:31, Romans 8:28, Galatians 6:9.
  • Identity in Christ — Ephesians 2:10, 2 Corinthians 5:17, John 1:12.

How we set scripture on a shirt

Typesetting decides whether a verse gets read or skimmed past. Long passages are broken at their natural clauses instead of at the edge of the print area, references sit on their own line beneath the text, and line spacing opens up as the passage gets longer so the block never turns into a wall.

  • Full passages, not fragments — the verse is printed through to its end.
  • Reference always included — book, chapter, and verse, so anyone can look it up.
  • Read at conversation distance — type sized for six feet away, not six inches.
  • Serif for narrative, grotesk for command — the typeface follows the tone of the passage.
  • Ink matched to weight — water-based on lighter cotton for a soft hand, discharge on darker garments.

Fabric, fit, and care

  • Classic cotton, 5.3oz — everyday weight with a relaxed drape.
  • Ring-spun combed, 4.2oz — softest hand and the sharpest verse type.
  • Heavyweight boxy, 7.0oz — structured body and dropped shoulder.
  • Sizes XS–3XL in a unisex cut, pre-shrunk and side-seamed.
  • Wash inside out, cold, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach or direct iron.

Bible verse t-shirt questions, answered

What is a Bible verse t-shirt?

A t-shirt printed with the full text of a scripture passage, usually with the book, chapter, and verse set alongside it. Ours use the American Standard Version and are laid out so the whole verse can be read at conversational distance, not just a fragment.

Which Bible translation do you print?

The American Standard Version (ASV). It is in the public domain, which means we can print full passages without licensing restrictions, and its wording stays close to the underlying Hebrew and Greek.

Which verses are available?

Verses are grouped by need rather than by book: courage, grief, anxiety, thanksgiving, identity, and endurance. Common requests include Joshua 1:9, Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 23, Philippians 4:6-7, Romans 8:28, Proverbs 3:5-6, and 2 Timothy 1:7.

Can you print a specific verse that is not listed?

Yes. Send the reference and the shirt weight and color you want, and we will set it in one of our existing verse layouts. Custom verse requests take an extra two to three business days.

Will a long passage still be readable?

Yes. Longer passages are set in a narrower measure at a larger point size, with the reference on its own line, so the block reads as a paragraph instead of shrinking to fit.

Are these good for church groups, small groups, or memorization?

They are widely used for memory-verse series, retreats, VBS weeks, and confirmation classes. Any single verse design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes.

What sizes and fabrics do Bible verse tees come in?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 5.3oz classic cotton, 4.2oz ring-spun combed, or 7.0oz heavyweight boxy. Fine-set verse type prints most crisply on the 4.2oz ring-spun.

How do I keep the printed text from cracking?

Wash inside out in cold water and tumble dry low or hang dry. No bleach, and never iron directly over the printed verse.
