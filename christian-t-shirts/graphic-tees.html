What is a Christian graphic tee?

The short answer

A graphic tee leads with the artwork rather than the words. These are our largest, most illustrated prints: full-front layouts, layered colour, drawn lettering, and designs built around a single image instead of a line of type. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

What is in this collection

  • Full-front prints — the design runs chest to hem rather than sitting as a small chest hit.
  • Illustrated scripture — the verse is drawn, not typeset.
  • Multi-colour layouts — two to five inks per design.
  • Heavyweight blanks — 7.0oz boxy cotton holds a large print flat.
  • Style series — the same message drawn in a dozen different treatments.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 5.3oz classic cotton, 4.2oz ring-spun combed, or 7.0oz heavyweight boxy. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no direct iron on the print. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Graphic Tees questions, answered

What makes a shirt a graphic tee?

The artwork carries the design. A graphic tee is built around an illustration or a drawn composition rather than a line of set type.

What blank do you print graphic tees on?

Mostly the 7.0oz heavyweight boxy cotton, which keeps a large front print flat and square across the chest.

How many colours are in a print?

Between two and five inks, depending on the design. Water-based, discharge or plastisol is chosen per artwork.

Do graphic tees run larger?

The heavyweight boxy cut runs wider through the chest and shoulder with a shorter body. Size down for a closer fit.

Can I order one design across mixed sizes?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set for a church, group or event.

The give-back
