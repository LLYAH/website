What is a Jesus t-shirt?

This collection puts the name and the person of Christ on the front of the shirt rather than a symbol or a slogan. Jesus, God, Lord, Savior, King. The names carry the weight, and the design is built to serve them rather than to compete with them.

Names and titles in this collection

  • Jesus — the name itself, set plainly and at scale.
  • Jesus Saves — the shortest complete statement of the gospel.
  • King of Kings — from Revelation 19:16.
  • Lion of Judah — from Revelation 5:5, drawn as type and as mark.
  • Alpha and Omega — from Revelation 22:13.

How the names are set

Setting a name is a different problem from setting a sentence. There is no context to carry a weak treatment, so type weight, spacing, and scale do all the work.

  • Full-chest type — the name at maximum readable scale.
  • Serif and blackletter — traditional weight for titles like King of Kings.
  • Minimal sans — the name set small and quiet on the chest.
  • Name with a reference — the title paired with where it comes from.
  • Back print — large-scale names across the shoulders.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 5.3oz classic cotton, 4.2oz ring-spun combed, or 7.0oz heavyweight boxy. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no direct iron on the print. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Jesus t-shirt questions, answered

Where does King of Kings come from?

Revelation 19:16, where the title is written on the robe and thigh of the returning Christ.

What does Lion of Judah mean?

A title for Christ from Revelation 5:5, drawing on Jacob's blessing over Judah in Genesis 49:9.

What does Alpha and Omega mean?

The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet, used in Revelation 22:13 as a title meaning the beginning and the end.

Which design is the most understated?

The minimal sans treatments, where the name is set small on the left chest rather than across the front.

What sizes and fits do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in three cotton weights.

Are these good for evangelism or outreach?

They are the most direct shirts in the shop. Jesus Saves in particular is the most ordered outreach design here.
