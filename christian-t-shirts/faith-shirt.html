What is a faith statement t-shirt?

A faith statement tee says something in the wearer's own words rather than quoting a passage. That is the distinction from a verse shirt: it is a declaration, not a citation. Faith Over Fear, Redeemed, Chosen, Not Today. Short lines that a person means before they wear them.

Statements in this collection

  • Faith Over Fear — the most requested line in the shop, in eighteen design styles.
  • Redeemed and Chosen — identity statements drawn from Ephesians and 1 Peter.
  • Saved by Grace — the shortest summary of the gospel that fits on a chest.
  • Walk by Faith — from 2 Corinthians 5:7, stated rather than quoted.
  • He Is Risen — the Easter statement, worn year round.

Design range

The same three words can be set eighteen different ways, and the style changes who wears it. A Bauhaus treatment and a graffiti treatment of Faith Over Fear reach entirely different people.

  • Bold streetwear — heavy condensed type across the full chest.
  • Minimal and Swiss — small, precise, understated.
  • Vintage and collegiate — arched varsity lettering with a distressed print.
  • Hand-drawn and doodle — loose personal letterforms.
  • Retro and Y2K — chrome, gradient, and period treatments.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 5.3oz classic cotton, 4.2oz ring-spun combed, or 7.0oz heavyweight boxy. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no direct iron on the print. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Faith statement t-shirt questions, answered

What is the difference between a statement tee and a verse tee?

A statement tee is written in the wearer's own words. A verse tee quotes scripture directly with its reference.

What is the most popular faith statement?

Faith Over Fear, by a wide margin. It is available here in eighteen distinct design styles.

Are these appropriate for a workplace?

The minimal and Swiss treatments are. The bold streetwear and graffiti versions read louder and suit casual settings.

Can I get the same statement in a different style?

Yes. Most statements are drawn in several treatments, so you can pick the visual language separately from the message.

What sizes do you carry?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in three cotton weights.

Can a small group order matching statement tees?

Yes. Any single design can be ordered as a set across mixed sizes.
