What is a funny Christian t-shirt?

The short answer

A funny Christian t-shirt uses humour as the opening line. The joke is the reason someone reads the shirt; the message is the reason it is worth wearing. Ours are written to be a conversation starter rather than a punchline at anyone else's expense. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

What is in this collection

  • Scripture puns — plays on verses people already know.
  • Church-life humour — potlucks, small groups, the front row.
  • Coffee and Jesus — the most requested category we make.
  • Youth group designs — written to survive a week of camp.
  • Small chest hits — quieter jokes for people who want one.

Fabric, fit, and care

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in 5.3oz classic cotton, 4.2oz ring-spun combed, or 7.0oz heavyweight boxy. Wash inside out in cold water, tumble low or hang dry, no bleach and no direct iron on the print. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. You are helping the Kingdom of Heaven with every purchase.

Funny Tees questions, answered

Are funny Christian shirts irreverent?

No. The humour is aimed at ourselves, not at God or at anyone else. Nothing here mocks scripture.

Are these appropriate for church or youth group?

Yes. Every design is written with a youth leader and a Sunday morning in mind.

What sizes do funny tees come in?

XS to 3XL in a unisex cut, in three cotton weights.

Do they make good gifts?

They are our most ordered gift category, especially the coffee designs.
