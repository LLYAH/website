What is a rocks glass?

A rocks glass is a short, wide, heavy-bottomed tumbler, usually 10 to 12oz. The weight is the point: it sits solidly in the hand and on a table, and the thick base gives an etched design a surface with real depth to it.

What they get used for

  • Water and juice at the table — the everyday short glass.
  • Old fashioned and neat pours — the shape the glass is named for.
  • Desk and nightstand glasses — stable, hard to knock over.
  • Gift pairs — two etched glasses in a box, with a date.
  • Retirement and ordination gifts — a name and passage on a weighted glass.

Weight, etching, and dimensions

The thick base refracts light through an etched design, which is why sandblast etching looks better on this shape than on any other glass in the collection.

  • 10oz and 12oz — 3.5in tall, 3.3in rim diameter.
  • Heavy weighted base — up to 0.75in of solid glass.
  • Sandblast etched — permanent, dishwasher safe.
  • Clear or smoke tint — smoke available on select designs.
  • Sold singly or in pairs. — undefined

Rocks glass questions, answered

How big is a rocks glass?

10 or 12oz, 3.5 inches tall with a 3.3 inch rim. It is deliberately short and wide.

Why is the base so thick?

Weight and stability, and the thick base refracts light through an etched design, which is what makes etching look best on this shape.

Is the etching dishwasher safe?

Yes. Sandblast etching removes glass rather than sitting on top of it, so it is permanent.

Can a name or date be added?

Yes on most designs, which is why etched pairs are the standard retirement and anniversary gift here.

Why does the etched area look cloudy after washing?

It is a textured surface holding water. It clears completely as it dries.

Are they sold as a pair?

Singly or in pairs. Pairs ship in a rigid gift box.
