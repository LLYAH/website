What is a glass wall hanging?

The short answer

A glass wall hanging is a stained or fused glass panel mounted against a wall rather than in a window. Without backlight it reads differently: the opalescent and textured glass carries the piece, and the color comes from the glass itself instead of from the sun behind it. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Where these work

  • Above a mantel — a horizontal panel as the room centerpiece.
  • Entry walls — a cross or verse panel at eye height.
  • Hallways — narrow vertical panels in a run of three.
  • Church lobbies and prayer rooms — larger panels sized to be read across a room.
  • Beside a window — catches side light without needing to hang in the glass.

Construction and mounting

A wall panel carries more weight than a suncatcher, so mounting matters. Every panel ships with standoff hardware that holds it a half inch off the wall, which lets ambient light pass behind and keeps the piece from reading flat.

  • Sizes — 10x14, 14x20, and 18x24 inches.
  • Copper foil and reinforced frame — zinc or brass channel around the edge.
  • Standoff mounting hardware — holds the panel half an inch off the wall.
  • Opalescent and textured glass — carries color without backlight.
  • Weight — two to seven pounds depending on size.

Hanging and care

Anchor into a stud or use a rated drywall anchor, since the largest panels reach seven pounds. Clean with a dry cloth only. Glass wall hanging questions, answered

Does a glass wall panel need a window behind it?

No. These use opalescent and textured glass that carries color under ordinary room light. Side light from a nearby window adds depth.

How is it mounted?

Standoff hardware holds it a half inch off the wall. Anchor into a stud or use a drywall anchor rated for the panel weight.

How heavy are the panels?

Two pounds at 10x14 up to about seven pounds at 18x24.

Can these be hung in a bathroom?

Yes. Humidity does not affect glass or solder. Avoid direct shower spray on the frame.

Can a church order a larger custom panel?

Yes, above 18x24 as a quote. Send the wall dimensions, the passage, and your palette.

How do I clean it?

Dry microfiber cloth only. Ammonia-based glass cleaner will corrode the solder lines.
