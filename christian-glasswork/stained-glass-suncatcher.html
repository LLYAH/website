What is a stained glass suncatcher?

The short answer

A suncatcher is a small stained glass panel hung in a window so that daylight passing through it throws colored light into the room. Unlike wall art, it changes through the day: strongest in direct morning or afternoon sun, quiet and jewel-like the rest of the time. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Designs in this collection

  • Cross suncatchers — the most requested shape, in amber, cobalt, and clear textures.
  • Dove and olive branch — soft whites and greens for a nursery or kitchen window.
  • Verse panels — a short reference sandblasted into one clear pane.
  • Geometric rose windows — circular panels drawn from cathedral traceries.
  • Memorial pieces — a name and date worked into a small hanging panel.

Construction and hanging

These are built with copper foil and solder rather than lead came, which is what allows the thinner lines and tighter curves that a small panel needs.

  • Copper foil construction — fine solder lines, no lead came.
  • Sizes — 4in, 6in, and 8in across.
  • Chain or suction hook — included, sized for a standard window.
  • Real stained glass — cathedral, opalescent, and textured art glass.
  • Soldered hanging loop — integrated into the frame, not glued on.

Where to hang it and care

An east or south window gives the strongest color throw. Hang it away from a door that slams, clean with a dry cloth only, and never use ammonia glass cleaner, which will dull the solder over time. 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit. Stained glass suncatcher questions, answered

Which window is best for a suncatcher?

East for morning light, south for the longest exposure through the day. Any window works, but direct sun is what throws color into the room.

Is this real stained glass?

Yes. Cathedral, opalescent, and textured art glass cut and assembled with copper foil and solder, not printed acrylic.

How do I hang it?

A chain and suction hook are included. For a permanent spot, a small cup hook in the window frame is more secure than suction.

How do I clean stained glass?

A dry or barely damp cloth. Never ammonia-based glass cleaner, which corrodes solder lines and dulls the patina.

Is it safe around children and pets?

Hang it above reach. The panels are sturdy but they are glass, and a hard knock will crack a pane.

Can you make a memorial piece with a name?

Yes. Names and dates can be sandblasted into a clear pane within most designs. Allow an extra week.
