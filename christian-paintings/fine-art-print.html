What is a fine art print?

The short answer

A fine art print is a reproduction made to archival standards: acid-free cotton rag paper, pigment inks rather than dye, and a color profile matched to the original artwork. The distinction from a poster is not sharpness, it is lifespan. A fine art print is made to still look correct in fifty years. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

What makes a print archival

  • Cotton rag paper — acid-free and lignin-free, so it will not yellow.
  • Pigment inks — ground pigment rather than dye, rated for decades of color hold.
  • Neutral pH throughout — including the backing board it ships against.
  • White border — a 1in margin for handling and for mounting under a mat.
  • Signed edition marks — on limited pieces, numbered in pencil.

Papers, sizes, and framing

Paper choice changes the image more than most people expect. Smooth matte holds fine type crisply. Textured rag gives painted work a surface that reads closer to the original.

  • Smooth matte rag, 285gsm — best for typographic and line work.
  • Textured cotton rag, 310gsm — best for painted and watercolor reproductions.
  • Sizes — 8x10, 11x14, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x36 inches.
  • 1in white border — standard on every size, for matting.
  • Ships flat or rolled — flat in rigid board to 16x20, rolled above.

Framing and care

Frame under UV glazing with an acid-free mat, and keep the print out of direct all-day sun. Handle by the border. Fine art print questions, answered

What is the difference between a fine art print and a poster?

Materials and lifespan. Fine art prints use acid-free cotton rag and pigment inks rated for decades. Posters use coated stock and will yellow and fade far sooner.

What does giclee mean here?

Giclee is the inkjet process used to make these prints. Every fine art print in this collection is a giclee on archival rag.

Which paper should I choose?

Smooth matte for typographic and lettering pieces. Textured rag for painted, watercolor, and oil reproductions.

Do prints come framed?

No. They ship unframed with a 1in white border, sized to standard frames and mats.

How long will the colors last?

Pigment inks on cotton rag are rated for many decades indoors under normal light, longer behind UV glazing.

How should I handle the print?

By the white border, with clean dry hands. Fingerprint oil on the image area is difficult to remove from matte rag.
