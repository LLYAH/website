What is a framed painting print?

A framed print arrives finished: printed, matted, glazed, and fitted in a solid wood frame with hanging hardware already on the back. Buying it framed usually costs less than buying the print and having the same frame and mat cut at a shop, and it removes the step most people never get around to.

What is included

  • Solid wood frame — black, white, natural oak, or walnut.
  • Acid-free mat — a 2in white mat on most sizes, or no mat on request.
  • Shatter-resistant glazing — acrylic rather than glass, safe to ship and to hang over a crib.
  • Archival print — pigment ink on matte rag inside the frame.
  • Hardware fitted — wire and bumpers installed before shipping.

Sizes and frame profiles

The listed size is the outside frame dimension, not the image. A 16x20 framed print holds an 11x14 image inside a 2in mat, which is the proportion that reads best on a wall.

  • Frame sizes — 11x14, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x30 inches outside.
  • Profile widths — 0.75in slim, 1.25in standard.
  • Four finishes — matte black, matte white, natural oak, walnut.
  • Mat options — 2in white mat, or full-bleed with no mat.
  • Hangs vertical or horizontal — hardware fitted for the orientation of the art.

Framed print questions, answered

Is the listed size the frame or the image?

The frame. A 16x20 framed print holds an 11x14 image inside a 2in mat.

Is the glazing glass or acrylic?

Shatter-resistant acrylic. It ships safely, weighs less on the wall, and is the right choice over a crib or in a childrens room.

Can I order it without a mat?

Yes. A full-bleed option prints the image to the frame edge, which suits bold typographic pieces.

Which frame finish should I pick?

Black for high-contrast and typographic work, natural oak for warm and earth palettes, white for light rooms, walnut for traditional interiors.

Does it arrive ready to hang?

Yes. Wire and bumpers are fitted before shipping.

Can I replace the print later and keep the frame?

Yes. The backing is spring-clipped rather than sealed, so the print can be swapped.
