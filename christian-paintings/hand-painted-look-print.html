What is a hand-painted look print?

The short answer

A hand-painted look print keeps the evidence of the hand in the work: uneven letterforms, visible brush edges, dry-brush gaps, and paint that stops short of the line. It reads as something made by a person rather than set on a screen, which is the whole appeal. Love & Love YAH designs each one in-house, makes it to order, and donates 10% of every purchase to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

What defines the style

  • Visible brush edges — no clean vector outline anywhere in the piece.
  • Hand lettering — letterforms drawn rather than typed, with natural variance.
  • Dry-brush texture — gaps where the brush ran out mid-stroke.
  • Limited palette — usually two or three colors mixed on a palette, not picked.
  • Off-register overlaps — colors that meet imperfectly, on purpose.

Substrate and sizes

This style needs a paper with tooth. Printed on smooth stock the brush texture flattens into what looks like a filter, and the illusion collapses.

  • Textured cotton rag, 310gsm — tooth that matches the painted original.
  • Canvas option — stretched at 1.25in for a fully painted read.
  • Sizes — 8x10, 11x14, 16x20, and 18x24 inches.
  • 1in white border — on paper prints, sized for standard mats.
  • Pigment inks — holds the soft edges without hard banding.

Where it works

This style suits kitchens, entryways, and farmhouse and cottage interiors, where a mechanically perfect print looks out of place. Hand-painted look print questions, answered

Is this actually hand-painted?

No. It is a print of hand-painted original artwork. Every brushstroke you see was made by hand before it was reproduced.

Why does the lettering look uneven?

Because it was drawn with a brush rather than set in a typeface. That variance is the style, not a printing fault.

Will it look like a print up close?

Up close you can see it is printed. At normal viewing distance the paper texture and brush edges read as paint.

Paper or canvas?

Canvas reads more convincingly as a painting. Paper under glass looks more deliberate and suits a gallery wall.

What rooms does this style suit?

Kitchens, entryways, and farmhouse or cottage interiors, where perfectly clean type looks out of place.

Can I get a set in the same palette?

Several designs come as coordinated sets of three in one palette, sized 8x10 or 11x14.
