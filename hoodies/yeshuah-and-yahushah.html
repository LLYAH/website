What are Yeshuah and Yahushah hoodies?

These hoodies use the Hebrew names rather than the Greek and English forms most English Bibles carry. Yeshuah and Yahushah transliterate the name rendered in English as Jesus; YAH and Yahweh render the divine name translated as LORD. For many believers, using the Hebrew name holds on to what translation smooths over.

Names in this collection

  • Yeshuah — a common transliteration of the Hebrew name of Christ.
  • Yahushah — a fuller transliteration used in Sacred Name congregations.
  • YAH — the shortened divine name, as in halleluYAH.
  • Yahweh — the four-letter divine name in English letters.
  • Paleo-Hebrew script — the name in the ancient letterforms.

Setting unfamiliar names on fleece

Most English readers have not seen these names in print, so tracking and weight matter more here than anywhere else. On fleece the letterspacing opens further still, since the nap closes tight gaps between letters.

  • Wide letterspacing — so the name is read, not guessed at.
  • Paleo-Hebrew and modern Hebrew script — alongside the transliteration.
  • Transliteration with the English form — both, one above the other.
  • Minimal chest placement — for everyday wear.
  • Full-back type — pullover only, at the largest scale.

Sacred Name hoodie questions, answered

What does Yeshuah mean?

It transliterates the Hebrew name rendered in English as Jesus. The Hebrew root carries the sense of salvation or deliverance.

What is the difference between Yeshuah and Yahushah?

Both transliterate the same name. Yahushah is a fuller rendering preserving more of the divine name YAH at the start, used most in Sacred Name congregations.

What does YAH mean?

The shortened form of the divine name, the same YAH at the end of halleluYAH.

What is paleo-Hebrew?

The older Hebrew script used before the square Aramaic letterforms of modern Hebrew. Several designs set the name in it.

Why use the Hebrew name instead of Jesus?

For many believers it holds to the name as originally spoken rather than as translation rendered it. Both forms are used across the wider church.

What sizes and weights do you carry?
