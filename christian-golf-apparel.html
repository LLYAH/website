Christian golf apparel that passes the dress code

The short answer

Christian golf apparel is faith-based golf wear built on performance fabric: moisture-wicking pique polos, quarter-zip layers, structured performance caps and course towels with embroidered or subtly printed Christian designs. Love & Love YAH makes its golf line to order in sizes S to 4XL, uses stitched embroidery rather than large chest prints so pieces stay collared-shirt appropriate, and donates 10% of every purchase.

Golf is four hours of walking and talking, which makes it one of the few places where a shirt has time to start a real conversation. The catch is the dress code. A large screen-printed graphic gets a look at most private clubs, so this collection is built the way golf apparel is actually built: performance fabric, clean collar, and the message carried in embroidery at the chest, sleeve or back yoke.

What is in the golf collection

  • Performance polos — moisture-wicking pique or jersey knit with four-way stretch, a self-fabric collar and three-button placket.
  • Quarter-zips — midweight brushed-back knit for cold mornings, cut to swing in.
  • Performance caps — structured and unstructured crowns, rope hats, and moisture-wicking sweatbands. See all hats.
  • Course accessories — embroidered towels and headcovers.
  • Layering pieces — long-sleeve sun shirts with UPF-rated fabric for summer rounds.

Fabric and performance

Polos use a polyester-spandex pique with a wicking finish that pulls sweat off the skin and dries fast in wind. That finish is the reason care instructions matter more here than anywhere else in the shop: fabric softener coats the fibers and kills the wicking, and high heat in the dryer can set odor into synthetic knits. Wash cold, dry low or hang, and the shirt performs for years.

  • Four-way stretch so the shoulder is free at the top of the backswing.
  • Wrinkle resistant — it comes out of a golf bag ready to wear.
  • UPF sun protection on long-sleeve styles.
  • Anti-odor finish on polos and caps.
  • No fabric softener, no high heat — the two things that ruin performance fabric.

Designs that stay course-appropriate

The golf designs are quiet on purpose. A small embroidered cross or ichthys at the left chest. A verse reference stitched on the sleeve — Philippians 4:13, Psalm 23, Joshua 1:9 — short enough to read on a follow-through. A phrase like fairways and faith across the back yoke where a playing partner sees it during your round. Caps carry the more direct designs, since headwear is where golf allows personality.

This is also the collection church leagues and men's ministry groups order most. One embroidered design across a whole team reads as a uniform rather than a statement, and it holds up over a season of scrambles.

Cold mornings and league play

Half the rounds most people play in spring and fall start below fifty degrees and finish in short sleeves, so the collection is built to layer down through a round. A long-sleeve sun shirt under a polo covers the first six holes, the quarter-zip comes off at the turn, and the polo carries the rest of the day. All three pieces are cut to swing in, which means a raglan or set-in sleeve that does not bind at the top of the backswing and a hem that stays put through a full follow-through.

For church leagues, scrambles and men's ministry outings, one embroidered design across mixed sizes is the order we run most. Send us a roster with sizes and we stitch the same left-chest mark on every shirt, add sleeve embroidery for a date or event name if you want it, and ship the whole set together. Lead time is the same two to four business days for groups under fifty shirts.

Sizing for golf shirts

Golf polos run true to size with an athletic cut through the chest and a straight hem meant to be tucked. If you play in a tucked shirt, order your true size. If you prefer it untucked or want room for a base layer in cold weather, size up one. Quarter-zips are cut to layer over a polo, so your normal size is right.

  • S to 4XL in polos and quarter-zips.
  • Sleeve length sits mid-bicep on standard polos.
  • Caps are one-size-adjustable with rope and fitted options.

Everything is printed or embroidered to order and ships in two to four business days. Free shipping over $75, thirty-day returns, and 10% of every purchase is donated to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.

Christian golf apparel questions, answered

What is Christian golf apparel?

Golf wear built on performance fabric with Christian designs: moisture-wicking polos, quarter-zips, embroidered caps and course accessories carrying scripture, crosses or short faith statements.

Will these polos pass a club dress code?

Yes. Designs are stitched embroidery at the chest, sleeve or back yoke with a proper collar and placket, rather than large screen-printed graphics.

What fabric are the golf polos?

A polyester-spandex pique with four-way stretch, a moisture-wicking finish, anti-odor treatment and wrinkle resistance.

How do I wash performance golf shirts?

Wash cold and dry low or hang dry. Avoid fabric softener, which clogs the wicking finish, and avoid high heat, which sets odor into synthetics.

Do the polos run true to size?

Yes, with an athletic cut and a straight tuckable hem. Size up one if you wear polos untucked or layer underneath.

Can you do church league or group orders?

Yes. One embroidered design across mixed sizes is the most common golf order we run.

The give-back

Every golf order sends 10% to a Christian ministry, mission, charity or non-profit.
