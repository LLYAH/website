Today · Day 237

The Quiet Obedience

Most obedience is unremarkable. It looks like showing up for the same job, forgiving the same person, and praying the same prayer without an audience. Naaman wanted a miracle worth telling; he was given a river and a count of seven. The healing waited on the seventh dip, not the first.

Ask yourself where you are stopping at six. The instruction has not changed and neither has the promise attached to it.

Reading

2 Kings 5:1–14 · ASV

Verse

"Go and wash in Jordan seven times, and thy flesh shall come again to thee."

Practice

Do the small thing you already know to do, today, without telling anyone.

This week · Week 34

Rest Is Not a Reward

Sabbath came before the work was finished, not after. God gave a day off to people who had just been freed from making bricks, and He gave it as a boundary, not a bonus. This week's reading traces rest from Genesis to the Gospels and asks a plain question: what would change if you stopped earning your rest?

Readings
